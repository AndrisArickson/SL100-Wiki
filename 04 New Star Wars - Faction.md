## CAREER PATH & FACTION

Your career path is a summary of what your character has done before your character started along their Star Wars journey. This path begins with your birth, and winds from your childhood to your place in the Faction's hierarchy as an adult. You can think of your career path as your path through life that has formed what you are to date but it does not define all that you can be.

The backstory of your character and what they have achieved to date in their career. Your Faction describes the focus of your life until you were recruited by your Patron. Some Factions search for specific attributes, such as force sensitivity to join a Force Tradition, for the Black Sun as an example it could be a myriad of things such as intelligence, persuasiveness, strength or other talents.

## FACTIONS


- **Force Traditions** — Light Side, Neutral / Grey and Dark Side
- **Governments, Militaries and Powers** — the Republic, the Separatists, the Empire, the Rebellion, the New Republic and their successors, their armed forces and intelligence services, and the Mandalorians
- **Criminal Underworld** — major crime syndicates; smugglers and pirate gangs; spice trafficking and slaving; bounty hunter guilds
- **Mega Corporations and Federations** — starship, weapons, clone and research corporations
- **Imperial Powers** — the shipyard commands, production bureaux and research programmes of the Empire

Each entry links to its Legends article. Entries marked *SL100 homebrew* have no Legends article and are inventions for this game. See *How Factions work* below.

*Source: Star Wars Legends continuity. Eras use the BBY/ABY dating standard.*

## How Factions work

A Faction is **who you work with**, not what you can do. It gives no Characteristics, Skills or Talents of its own.

- **Second-order benefits:** the contacts, safe houses, jobs, transport, gear, information and favours that come from belonging, and the doors your Faction's name opens.
- **Third-order detriments:** your Faction's enemies become your enemies, its obligations call on you at the worst moment, and its reputation (good or bad) arrives before you do.
