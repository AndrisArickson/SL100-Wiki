**Dun Moch**

Dun Moch is a Dark Side technique, able to be used and taught by Jedi (secretly) or Dark Side traditions such as Sith and Night Sisters

A practitioner must have more than 8 dark side influence to use it. Using it, has the potential to be further influenced by the Dark Side of the Force.

Each combat in which it is is used both the initiate/practitioner and the target of this technique undertake a Cool test with scaling difficulty as determined by GM or gain Dark Side Influence. 

Gains:

1 Dark Side Influence at Initiate 

4 Dark Side Influence a Practitioner 

8 Dark Side Influence at Master

***Where the Jedi seek peace before the blade is drawn, the Sith seek collapse before it ever lands a blow. Dun Moch is war waged in two registers at once — the clash of sabers and the quieter, crueller war fought in the space behind an opponent's eyes. To duel a Dun Moch practitioner is to discover that your worst enemy in the fight was never standing across from you. It was already inside your own head, waiting to be found.***
