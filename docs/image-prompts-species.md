# Star Legends species portraits: image-generation prompts

223 playable species of *Star Legends - Galactic Wars*. Each prompt was written from the Legends (Wookieepedia) article held in the SL100 wiki, so the look follows the Expanded Universe, not later canon. Pass this file whole, or one block at a time, to the image generator. Save each result under the **Save as** path inside `SL100-Wiki/`: that is the file the species' Legends article shows in its infobox, so the picture replaces the blank placeholder on both the article and the species index. Keep the file extension given (convert if the generator outputs another format).

## Global style block (prepend to every prompt)

> Illustration for a science-fantasy tabletop roleplaying game species guide, in the look of the 1990s–2000s Star Wars Expanded Universe sourcebooks: painted, grounded, slightly weathered "used future" realism. One single adult individual, full body visible from head to feet, three-quarter view, standing on a plain floor against a neutral mid-grey gradient background. Soft directional studio lighting, natural colours, fine surface detail on skin, fur, scales and cloth. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no frame. No other figures, no weapons pointed at the viewer.

## Global avoid list (append to every prompt as negatives)

> text, caption, watermark, signature, logo, insignia, multiple characters, named film character likeness, cartoon or anime styling, chibi proportions, cropped limbs, cluttered background, lens flare, extra limbs beyond those described, human face on a non-human species.

## How the blocks are built

Every block has the species name, the Legends article it was written from, the file to save as, a **Prompt** (the species description, 90–150 words, in a fixed order: height against a human, build, head and face, skin or fur or scales, hands and feet, tail or wings, clothing and gear typical of the species in the Legends era, pose) and an **Avoid** line naming the mistakes a generator is likely to make for that species. Blocks marked *thin source* were written from an article that had little or no physical description; compare those with the article's artwork before accepting the picture.
