# Review: SL100 chapters 06 (Skills), 07 (Talents), Talent List, 08 (Equipment), Special Materials

Read-only review of five drafts under `/mnt/user-data/uploads/SL100/SL100-Wiki/`, cross-checked against `01 Introduction & Characteristics.md` and `05 Careers.md` for the characteristic-model question only.

---

## 1. `06 New Star Wars - Skills.md`

### (a) Contents and completeness

**Count.** The "Complete Skill List" names **26 Basic** and **19 Advanced** = **45 skills**. The Master Skill List has **44 entries**; **Use the Force has no entry at all**.

**New vs renamed.** This is a rename pass over WFRP 4e, not an expansion. Renamed: Pilot (Speeder) ← Drive (text still has "One banthas breaks free", Broken Wheel/Axle mishap table); Pilot (Starship) ← Row (literally "Your prowess at pulling an oar", truncated); Pilot (Capital Ship) ← Sail (the Sail text "knotwork, gauging the wind" is orphaned under Research); Slice Security System ← Pick Lock (lock table with "Treasure Chest"); Demolitions ← Set Trap; Codes (Faction) ← Secret Signs; Meditate ← Pray ("Chapter 7: Religion & Belief" left in); Profession ← Trade; Consume Intoxicant ← Consume Alcohol (body still says "Consume Alcohol Test" ×4); Creature Care/Training ← Animal Care/Training; Medicine ← Heal; Language (The Force) ← Language (Magick); Use the Force ← Channelling (name only). There is no genuinely new skill: computer intrusion is buried in Profession (Slicer) while "Slice Security System" is physical lockpicking.

**Characteristic per skill.** Int ×10 (Codes, Creature Care/Training, Evaluate, Gamble, Language, Lore, Medicine, Outdoor Survival, Research); Ag ×8 (Athletics, Dodge, Perform, three Pilot, Ride, Stealth); Fel ×7 (Bribery, Charm, Entertain, Gossip, Haggle, Leadership, Meditate); **Dex ×6** (Art, Demolitions, Slice Security System, Play, Profession, Sleight of Hand); I ×4 (Intuition, Navigation, Perception, Track); S ×3 (Climb, Intimidate, Swim); WP ×2 (Charm Creature, Cool); T ×2 (Consume Intoxicant, Endurance); WS (Melee); BS (Ranged). Unassigned: Use the Force.

**Descriptions and examples.** 42 of 44 entries have usable text, most with a combat-use paragraph. Truncated/garbled: Lore (cuts off "in order for the"; the rest sits above Leadership), Perception (ends "beyond the immediately obvious about"), Pilot (Starship) (ends "automatically presumed"), Charm (Begging/Public Speaking options scattered across Bribery, a stray `### Begging` heading and Charm). Examples keep WFRP names (Adhemar, Snorri, Lord Ludwig von Schemp) and references (watchman, footpads, Hydra, bagpipes).

**Specialisation lists.** Present for all 14 grouped skills, but the Complete Skill List and the entries **disagree**: Melee is "(1H/2H/\*Lightsaber)" in the list but "Brawling, Vibroblade, Vibro-Axe, Force Pike, Lightsaber, Electrostaff, Stun Baton, Parry, Two-Handed" in the entry; Ranged "(Archaic/Blaster/Heavy/Ordinance)" vs "Blaster Pistol, Blaster Rifle, Heavy Blaster, Slugthrower, Bowcaster, Disruptor, Grenade/Explosives, Missile/Rocket, Sniper"; Stealth "(Urban/Wilderness)" vs "Rural, Underground, Urban". Chapter 08's tables use a third scheme (Ranged (Archaic)/(Blaster), Melee (1H)/(2H)/(\*Lightsaber)).

### (b) Design decisions evident

- Lightsabers are a Melee specialisation with a flat **–20 for non-Force-sensitives**, not a separate skill.
- Three Pilot skills: Basic (Speeder, Starship) and Advanced grouped (Capital Ship: Mega Freighter/Capital Ship/Space Station).
- Language/Lore (The Force) replace Magick; Meditate replaces Pray and points at "Force traditions".
- **Dexterity is retained** as a tenth characteristic (six skills). Chapter 01 says "nine Characteristics" (WS/BS/S/T/Ag/Int/I/WP/Fel); Chapter 05 career tables are ten-column with Dex. **06 follows the 10-characteristic WFRP 4e model and contradicts Chapter 01.**
- The opening paragraphs ("green dice… Purples and Reds… Breach") are FFG/Genesys narrative-dice musings, not d100.

### (c) Inconsistencies, placeholders, duplicates

- **Duplicate blocks**: Ride, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Shadowing, Swim and Track appear twice (lines 335–361, again bold at 363–388).
- **Ranged** is Basic in the list but headed "advanced, grouped".
- Pilot (Speeder): "Profession (Mechanic) or Profession (Mechanic)"; "Pilot (Land Vehicle) Test".
- Author's note left in-line: "Replace Battle Tongue with Military Cant or Hand Sign"; Climb has stray "3-4 / 5-6" prefixes.
- All page/chapter references ("page 164", "Chapter 11: Consumers' Guide", "page 190") are WFRP pagination; Research requires "Datapad Literacy Talent" (07 calls it Datapad Literate).

### (d) Gaps for a complete chapter

1. Write **Use the Force** (or move it to Chapter 09 and drop it from the list).
2. Replace Row/Sail/Drive text with real starship/speeder/capital-ship rules; one specialisation scheme for Melee/Ranged/Stealth shared with 08.
3. Resolve Dex with Chapter 01; strip Genesys notes; de-duplicate; fix truncations; re-point page refs; Star Wars examples; a skill-by-characteristic summary table.

## 2. `07 New Star Wars - Talents.md` and `Talent List.md`

### (a) Contents and completeness

**Count in 07**: **167 `###` entries** — 148 general talents (Accurate Shot … Well-Prepared, including Bookish whose heading is split over two lines) plus 19 "Force Talents", one of which (Force Specialty) is a pick-list framework rather than a talent.

**Count in Talent List.md**: **165 bullet lines**; **3 are duplicates** (Combat Aware, Attractive, Lip Reading each appear in two categories) and **20 are Force Specialties/Combined Powers**, sub-options rather than talents. Net: **142 unique talent names**.

**Reconciliation.**
- In 07 but **absent from Talent List (26)**: Amphibious, Eidetic Memory, Regeneration (Minor), Strike to Injure, Strike to Stun, Strong Legs, Strong Swimmer, Strong-Minded, Sturdy, Suave, Super Numerate, Supportive, Sure Shot, Surgery, Tenacious, Tinker, Trapper, Trick Riding, Very Resilient, Very Strong, War Leader, Warrior Born, Wealthy, Well-Prepared, Force Specialty, and Force Resistance (listed under its WFRP name **Magic Resistance**). Almost everything alphabetically after "Strike Mighty Blow" is missing — the summary was generated from an earlier draft and never refreshed; Amphibious, Eidetic Memory and Regeneration (Minor) are later additions.
- In Talent List but **not in 07**: Magic Resistance (rename) plus the 20 Force specialty powers (Projective Telepathy … Cleanse Soul), which 07 lists only as bullets under Force Specialty.
- The **Random Talents** d100 table references **Animal Affinity** (07: Creature Affinity) and **Read/Write** (07: Datapad Literate).

**Max / Tests / Description.** All but two have a Max line: **Regeneration (Minor)** (no Max or Tests, written in bestiary voice "the creature…") and **Force Specialty**. 47 lack a Tests line, normal for WFRP passive talents; **Secret Identity** has "Max: Intelligence" (missing "Bonus"). Every entry has a description, but several are broken: **Briber** has Cardsharp's whole text pasted into it; **Impassioned Zeal** contains the Inspiring table and example while **Inspiring** says "refer to the following table" with none; **Criminal** cuts off at "For the purposes of securing"; **Creature Affinity** and **Hyperlane Pilot** both contain a stray pasted line "**restore a limb in 1d10 days & 1 wound / hour**"; **Pilot** has a sentence fragment; **Speedreader** retains an editorial note "(make singular if previous suggestion is taken)"; **Frenzy** keeps the Flagellant's self-flagellation clause; **Jump Up** says Agility Test then Athletics Test.

**Dexterity** appears in 10 talents (Artistic, Crack the Whip, Craftsman, Fast Hands, Heavy Gunner, In-fighter, Master (Profession), Nimble Fingered, Rapid Reload, Tinker) — again a 10-characteristic assumption.

**Force-related talents (19)**: Dark Magic (Lore), Detect Force Artefact, Force Attunement, Force Magic (Lore), Force Resistance, Force Sensitive (Tradition), Force Sensitive (Sight), Force Ability – Alter/Control/Sense, Force Specialty, Force Visions, Force Fury, Foretold, Instinctive Diction, Magical Sense, Petty Magic, Pure Soul, Use The Force (Tradition); Pain-Sworn also references the Dark Side. **Two incompatible Force models coexist**: (i) a straight WFRP spell model — Channel Tests, Miscast on doubles, Language (Magick), Petty Magic with WP-Bonus spell slots, Force Magic (Lore) XP tables, "Arcane/Bless/Invoke/Witch!" talents; and (ii) a new WEG-style **Sense/Control/Alter** discipline model with universal powers, a Force Specialty rank-buy and combined-discipline powers (Battle Meditation C+S+A etc.). Force Sensitive (Tradition) is truncated at "If you call on the"; Force Magic (Lore) at "Dathomerian Witch,". Force Ability – Sense has "Max: Intuition Bonus" (Intuition is a skill, not a characteristic); Alter keyed to Fellowship Bonus needs justification against Chapter 09.

### (b) Design decisions evident

- Talents are near-verbatim WFRP 4e with name swaps and Star Wars examples (Fearless (Stormtroopers…), Strider (Vacuum, Gas Giant), Etiquette (Imperials, Hutts, Rebels)).
- New or rewritten: **Noble Blood (Galactic Aristocracy)** (full rewrite with benefit/restriction/flavour), **Amphibious**, **Eidetic Memory**, **Regeneration (Minor)**, **Heavy Gunner**, **Hyperlane Pilot** (River Guide), **Spacer**, **Vacuum Legs**, **Datapad Literate**, **Force Fury**; Resistance adds Radiation/Asphyxiation; Wealthy re-based to credits ("+1 GC (1000C)").
- Force disciplines deliberately diverge from WFRP magic — the big structural departure.

### (c) Inconsistencies and leftovers

- WFRP Old World residue: "Nordland… Ostland" (Blather), "fury of Ulric" (Furious Assault), "silver shillings" (Dealmaker), "brass pennies… gold crowns" (Embezzle), "Abbess… Greenskins" (Impassioned Zeal), "Trade (Apothecary)" (Concoct), "non-magical Trade Skills" (Tinker), "Heal Tests" (Surgery), "Melee (Fencing)" (Feint — not an SL specialisation), "Winds of Magic" (Magical Sense), "Chapter 8: Magic" (Petty Magic), "Set Trap" (Trapper).
- Talent List's "SOCIAL STANDING TALENTS" bucket (Ambidextrous, Hatred, Creature Affinity…) is mis-categorised; 21 "see page N" references are WFRP pagination.

### (d) Gaps

1. Regenerate Talent List.md from 07 (26 missing, 3 duplicates, Magic→Force Resistance, re-label the 20 specialty powers).
2. Pick one Force model; convert or delete Petty Magic/Instinctive Diction/Force Magic (Lore)/Dark Magic/Magical Sense; finish Sense/Control/Alter (Tests, costs, Max for Force Specialty).
3. Repair the ~12 broken entries; fix Random Talents names; currency and skill-name sweep.

## 3. `08 New Star Wars - Equipment.md`

### (a) Contents

**Categories**: stat key and reach/range key; **Pistol** (10), **Long Gun** (12), **Heavy Weapon** (10), **Brawling** (2), **Melee (1H)** (16, incl. Lightsaber, Lightwhip), **Melee (2H)** (7, incl. Great/Double Lightsaber), **Thrown** (11 grenades), **Engineering** (1), an orphaned **Kyber Crystal Colour d100** table, **Weapon Modifications** (7), **Armour** (location table, 14 rows), armour Qualities/Flaws (Flexible, Weakpoints, Cortosis Weave, Powered, Space, Camouflage), **Materials rank list**, an unstructured **Economy notes** dump (prices, currency 1 bronze=10c / silver=100c / gold=1000c, salaries, bounties), two further **armour tables** (16 and 30 rows), **Force Fields** (2), **General Equipment** (36), **Cybernetics** (1), **Genetic Engineering** (5).

**Weapon columns**: Name | Skill | Dam | Range | Mag | Enc | Cost | Avail | Traits | Description | Special Notes. Armour (first table): Price | Enc | Avail | Penalty | Loc | AP – Resist | Qual/Flaw | Material. Armour (later tables): Location | Armour | Enc | Cost | Avail | Traits.

**Filled vs placeholder.** Roughly 60% of weapon cells are filled. Blank or placeholder: Disruptor Pistol Dam "XX"; Ion Pistol Dam blank; Hold-out, Heavy Blaster Pistol, Sporting, Ion Pistol, Disruptor, Blaster Carbine, Heavy Blaster Rifle lack Cost and/or Mag/Range; Rocket Launcher, Missile Launcher, Repeating Blaster and PLX have **every stat blank** except Avail; Ion and Proton Grenade entirely blank; six melee weapons (Vibrosword, Electrostaff, Lightwhip, Cortosis Blade, Ryyk Blade, Gaderffii) and all four Beskar pieces have no Cost. Lightsaber Traits say "Penetrating (6)", Special Notes "Penetrating (?)". The **Description/Special Notes columns are imported from d20 Saga Edition** — "2-square line", "Reflex Defence", "Reflex DC 15 for half", "Fortitude DC 15", "Rifle proficiency", "Exotic Weapon feat", "full-round action", "flat-footed" — none of which exist in d100.

**Modifications**: 7 rows with cost/avail/used-with only, no mechanical effect, referencing undefined "Blast" and "Flamer" traits. **Cybernetics**: one entry, "Iron Skin" (a WFRP mutation). **Genetic Engineering**: five mutation-style entries (Lashing Tentacle, Inhuman Beauty, Horns, Claws, Regeneration) using "Ineffective Trait" and "Rend (1)" (Wrath & Glory terms); Regeneration duplicates the Regeneration (Minor) talent in 07.

### (b) Design decisions evident

- **Dual armour value "AP – Resist"** (Armour Points vs blasters/kinetic, Resist vs lightsabers) — the central new idea and the hook into Special Materials.
- **Material-based armour construction**: base pieces × material rank, "each rank doubles the cost"; Flexible layers under hard armour.
- Lightsaber damage = **6 + Form Bonus** (not SB), with Shield(2)/(3) and Penetrating.
- **Mag** column added; **Force Fields** with Protection/Overload; **Unique** tier above Exotic; credits replace GC/SS/BP; era-awareness ("Bacta is not available at 3666 BBY").

### (c) Inconsistencies, placeholders, duplicates

- **No Qualities & Flaws glossary**: 23 traits are used and none defined (Damaging, Hack, Pummel, Impale, Impact, Penetrating (X), Solid Projectile, Spread, Slow, Rapid Fire 4, Entangle, Undamaging, Defensive, Fast, Reach, Lightsaber Resistant, Shield (X), Inflict (X), Damages Electrics, Fine 3, Lightweight, Fitted, Survive in Space).
- Range notation mixed (S/M/L/E letters vs "Long"/"Extreme" words), no yardage per band; damage mixed "+StrB" vs "+SB"; Needle Rifle and Webber have "Melee (Two-Handed)" in Traits instead of 2H; Vibro-axe sits in the 1H table but is described as two-handed; **Electrostaff appears in both melee tables** with different stats; Double Lightsaber "Penetrating (7," unclosed.
- Kyber Colour table: **Green 36–70 overlaps Yellow 61–75**, and the table sits under "Engineering".
- Armour: three tables with incompatible columns; the 16-row table is a strict subset of the 30-row one; Plastoid AP "1 – 0" vs Materials list "Plastoid: Flaw 2"; Steel "3 – 1" vs Special Materials "3 – 0"; a nameless "Vest | Body | 5"; Beskar Breastplate "Torso" vs "Body".
- General Equipment uses **Dark Heresy skill names**: "Medicae test", "Tech(Security)", "Tech(Engineering)", "Awareness", "Augmentics"; distances in metres while weapons use yards/squares.
- Typos: "Supressor", "genetice", "Unnamed attacks", "Megopolis". Economy notes are research, not rules — move to an appendix.

### (d) Gaps

1. A **Qualities & Flaws** glossary (WFRP p.297 equivalent) and range bands in yards.
2. Fill blank cells; rewrite Special Notes in d100 terms; one armour schema; one specialisation scheme matching 06.
3. A real **Cybernetics** list (limbs, implants, cost/Enc/Avail, surgery rules) and a decision on what Genetic Engineering is (species trait, mutation, or purchasable augmentation).
4. Ammunition/power-pack economy, services, trappings by career, encumbrance limits, droid hand-off.

## 4. `Special Materials.md`

### (a) What it is and how it hooks in

A **materials reference**, not a rules chapter: an **Availability-by-settlement** table (Common→Unique × Outpost/Spaceport/City/Megopolis, replacing WFRP's Village/Town/City); definitions of Encumbrance, **Resist** (lightsaber DR) and **Armour Points** (blaster/kinetic DR); tables of **Lightest materials** (cloth/weave), **Metal alloys**, **Gaseous**, **Crystals** (12 lightsaber crystals + Kaiburr), **Organic/biological** (bacta, kolto, three spices priced per dose, Vong biotech, ysalamiri, hides), and **Camouflage/concealment** with prices. Each metal has a sourced prose entry with a Wookieepedia link — good provenance practice.

It hooks into Equipment via (i) the "AP – Resist" pair armour rows inherit from their Material column, (ii) 08's materials rank list (Plastoid→Beskar), (iii) the Availability tiers, (iv) crystals feeding the kyber table.

### (b) Design decisions

Settlement-scaled availability percentages; Uncommon and Unique tiers added to WFRP's four; the material, not the garment, defines AP/Resist/Weight; lightsaber-resistance restricted to Cortosis, Phrik, Beskar (plus Sith steel, Songsteel, Vong biotech); spice as priced consumable; crystals all Exotic, zero Enc.

### (c) Inconsistencies

- **Values disagree with Equipment**: Steel 3–0 here, 3–1 in 08; Phrik 5–5 here but Phrik Breastplate "Armour 6" in 08; Beskar 6–6 vs Beskar Breastplate 8; Rancor Hide 1–0 vs Rancor's Hide Vest 5; Durasteel "4" with no Resist; **Plastoid and Trimantium** are used by 08 armour but absent here.
- Verpine ultramesh "1–1 (3–3 charged)" in the table but "AP 3 – R 3" in prose; Quadanium "5 – Resist 1" vs 08's "Rank 2 cost improvement" with no stats.
- **Alchemical Sith Steel row duplicated** (line 108 and a stray one-row table at 251–253).
- Uncommon tier appears here and in 08's armour intro but in no weapon table.
- Crystals have no mechanics (Damind "increases precision", Opila "increases damage" — no numbers).
- Kolto "Rare" / bacta "Common" contradicts the 3666 BBY note unless era-tagged; Neuranium 15–10 weight 20 needs a "not wearable" rule.

### (d) Gaps

1. One authoritative **material → AP/Resist/Weight/Cost-multiplier/Availability** table that 08 references by name (add Plastoid, Trimantium).
2. Mechanical effects for lightsaber crystals; a crafting/forging rule (Profession (Weapons Technician) + material + time).
3. Era tags (Old Republic / Clone Wars / GCW / NJO) on availability, since the file spans 3666 BBY to NJO materials.
