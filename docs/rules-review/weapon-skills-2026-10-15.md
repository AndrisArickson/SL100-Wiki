# Weapon skills and career pass (15 Oct 2026)

Changes made to `05 New Star Wars - Careers (revised).md`, `03 New Star Wars - Origin.md`, `06 New Star Wars - Skills.md`,
`08 New Star Wars - Equipment.md` and `02 New Star Wars - Species (revised).md` on 15 Oct 2026.

## Rules applied

1. **Weapon groups.**
   - Melee (Basic): 1H, 2H, Brawling, Lightsaber.
   - Ranged (Advanced): Archaic, Blaster, Exotic, Ordnance, Thrown.
   - Choices are written with slashes in that order, e.g. `Melee (1H/2H)` or `Ranged (Blaster/Ordnance)`.
   - The player picks one group and writes that single skill on the character.
   - `Melee (Any)` / `Ranged (Any)` in careers became themed slash lists.
   - Lightsaber is offered only by careers with a Force tradition.
2. **Ranged first.**
   - Every martial career has Ranged (Blaster), or a choice that includes it, at level 1.
   - Every other career has it at level 1 or 2, except the exceptions below.
3. **Ranged (Ordnance)** is offered somewhere in every career with piloting or starship-combat skills.
4. **Melee (Lightsaber)** is offered at level 1 or 2 by the Force careers that fight with Force weapons.
5. **Generous melee choices** in melee careers.
6. **Level 1** lists 10 skills, plus 2 empty slots for skills from special circumstances (stated in the 05 intro).
7. **Datapad Use** is removed from every career's talents and replaced with a talent that fits the career.
   - Datapad Use comes from species and origin.
   - Uncivilised-world characters learn it in downtime or at character creation.
8. **Conversion notes** were accepted and their alternatives taken (see [conversion-notes-accepted.md](conversion-notes-accepted.md)).
   - Status tags are now all `[sl100]`.
   - `[converted]` markers were removed.
9. **Count fixes.**
   - Smuggler 2 had 7 skills (Gossip dropped).
   - Bounty Hunter 4 had 1 skill (Navigation added).
   - Bounty Hunter 1 had 5 talents (Pilot dropped).
   - Force Warrior 2 had 3 talents (Combat Aware added).

**No Ranged (Blaster) at levels 1-2 (deliberate):**
- Force Mystic, Force Savant, Hedge Adept, Monastic, Seer: spiritual or scholarly Force careers.
- Pit Fighter: arena melee career; its level 4 offers Ranged (Archaic/Blaster/Exotic/Thrown).

## Other chapters

- **03 Origin:**
  - `Melee (Any)` became `Melee (1H/2H/Brawling)` on Feudal, Feral, Industrial, Hostile, Agri and Ocean worlds.
  - `Ranged (Blaster or Ordnance)` became `Ranged (Blaster/Ordnance)` (Space).
  - Ranged (Blaster) was added where it was missing:
    - Feral: Ranged (Archaic/Blaster) replaces Navigation.
    - Ocean: Ranged (Archaic) became Ranged (Archaic/Blaster).
    - Tundra: Ranged (Archaic/Blaster) replaces Intimidate.
    - Force Nexus: Ranged (Blaster) replaces Endurance.
- **06 Skills:**
  - The Melee and Ranged lists are in slash form.
  - Ordnance covers large, heavy, tripod and turreted weapons, and every vehicle and starship weapon.
  - The E-Web and mounted repeating blasters moved from Blaster to Ordnance.
  - Blaster is the skill for any futuristic personal weapon.
- **08 Equipment:**
  - A Skill column was added to the Heavy Weapon, Melee (2H) and Thrown tables.
  - "Heavy Weapon (Ranged Heavy Weapon skill)" became "Heavy Weapon", all Ranged (Ordnance).
  - "Thrown (Ranged Archaic)" became "Thrown", all Ranged (Thrown).
  - Skills were aligned with 06:
    - Hand Flamer, Backpack Flamer, Needle Pistol, Needle Rifle, Web Pistol and Webber are Ranged (Exotic).
    - Bowcaster is Ranged (Archaic).
    - Vibro-axe is Melee (2H).
- **02 Species:** the Thyrasian Echani line reads Ranged (Ordnance).
