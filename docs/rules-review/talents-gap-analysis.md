# Talents gap analysis: Star Legends vs WFRP 4e (core and supplements)

2026-10-12. Compares the 167 talents in `07 New Star Wars - Talents (revised).md` with (a) the 165 talents of the WFRP 4e Rulebook, taken from the extracted text in `WFRP_Text/`, and (b) the talents published in WFRP 4e supplements, assembled from online lists (sources at the end). Supplement rule text could not be read online, only names and in some cases the gist, so the adaptation notes for those are proposals to check against the books if you have them.

Seven core talents are not captured. Each is small, and three of them are worth adding:

| Core talent | WFRP rule (gist) | Recommendation for Star Legends |
|---|---|---|
| **Strong Back** | Max Strength Bonus; Tests Row and Swim. Add your level to SL in Opposed Strength Tests, and carry that many extra Encumbrance points. | **Add** as *Strong Back* (Tests: Athletics and Swim). Distinct from Sturdy (which raises the Encumbrance limit by level × 2) because of the Opposed Strength bonus: grapples, wrestling a Gamorrean, holding a blast door. |
| **Tunnel Rat** | Max Initiative Bonus; Tests Stealth (Underground). Reverse a failed Stealth (Underground) Test if that scores a success. | **Add** as *Duct Crawler* (or keep Tunnel Rat). Maintenance shafts, Coruscant undercity, Sullustan warrens, mines of Kessel. Completes the set with Alley Cat (Urban) and Rover (Rural). |
| **Witch!** | Max 1. The gift of magic with no training: you know Willpower Bonus petty spells and one arcane spell of any lore, cast without Language (Magick), with heavier miscast and Corruption risks. | **Add** as *Wild Talent*. An untrained Force-sensitive who manifests power raw: one universal discipline power and Willpower Bonus minor effects, used on Willpower instead of Use the Force, with every double drawing Dark Side Influence. Exactly the self-taught Force user of Legends (Ventress before Dooku, Kyp Durron, the Witches of Dathomir's outcasts). |
| **Unshakable** | Max Toughness Bonus; Tests Endurance. Resist being knocked over, pushed back or moved against your will. | Optional. Fits Wookiees and heavy troopers; low priority because Jump Up and Iron Jaw cover most of the ground. |
| **Waterman** | Max Agility Bonus; Tests Sail (river craft). Ignore penalties from rolling water, swaying decks and poor footing on a boat; count as two crew. | Optional. Spacer already covers the starship version. Only needed if ocean worlds (Mon Calamari, Naboo, Kamino) get play; then *Boat Handler*. |
| **Fisherman** | Max Initiative Bonus; Tests any fishing. Feed yourself and level others from a large enough body of water. | Skip, or fold into Hunter's Eye as a speciality. |
| **Holy Hatred** | Max 1 (requires Hatred of your cult's enemy). Your Hatred extends to all enemies of your god; +1 SL on Melee Tests against them. | Skip in that form; Hatred (Group) already lists Sith, Imperials and so on. If the Force traditions want it, *Sworn Enemy (Tradition)*: Hatred against everything your tradition names as its foe. |

## 2. Supplement talents found online

These are the non-core talents listed in the fan compilations (World Anvil's WFRP talent index, the WFRP 4e wikidot page, and the character sheets visible in previews of Archives of the Empire I and II). The books they come from are given where known; where the rule is not readable online the gist is from memory of the book and is marked (*unverified*).

| Supplement talent | Book | Gist | Star Legends use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **¡¡Despierta, Fierro!!** | Up in Arms (Estalian duellists) | Draw and strike in one motion; initiative edge at the start of a duel (*unverified*). | **High value**: becomes *Quick Draw* for gunslingers and duellists. Star Wars has no faster archetype than the blaster drawn first. Pair with the Dual Wielding Blaster form. |
| **Lightning Strike** | Up in Arms | Act before others / strike first when combat opens (*unverified*). | **High value**: *First Strike* for Echani and lightsaber duellists, or merge with Quick Draw as the melee version. |
| **Art of War** | Up in Arms | Leadership and tactics; improves the party's or unit's Advantage or initiative when you command (*unverified*). | **Add** as *Tactician*: clone commanders, Rebel cell leaders, Mandalorian field marshals. Sits beside War Leader and Inspiring. |
| **Crew Commander** | Up in Arms (artillery crews) | Lets a crew-served weapon's crew act together more effectively under your orders (*unverified*). | **Add** as *Gunnery Chief*: turret gunners and capital-ship batteries. Needed once ship combat exists. |
| **Dog of War** | Up in Arms (mercenaries) | Mercenary's talent: better pay and standing with employers, Earning bonus (*unverified*). | **Add** as *Mercenary* or *Hired Gun*: bounty hunters and soldiers of fortune; a Dealmaker for fighting contracts. |
| **Vendetta** | Up in Arms (Tilean) | Hatred focused on one individual or family rather than a group, with stronger effects (*unverified*). | **Add** as *Blood Feud*: Mandalorian clan feuds, Hutt rivalries, the hunter who never lets a mark go. |
| **War Dance** | Up in Arms / Tribes and Tribulations | Ritual pre-battle dance granting Advantage or Psychology immunity (*unverified*). | Fits the Echani and Teras Kasi forms as a tier ability rather than a talent; otherwise *Battle Ritual* for Tusken Raiders and Wookiee berserkers. |
| **Warpaint** | Tribes and Tribulations (Greenskins) | Paint or markings that frighten enemies or steady the wearer (*unverified*). | *Clan Markings*: Mandalorian armour colours, Tusken war paint, Sith tattoos. Intimidate or Cool bonus while worn. |
| **Dead Men Tell No Tales** | Sea of Claws (pirates) | Intimidation and ruthlessness at sea; Fear effects or bonus against surrendering foes (*unverified*). | *No Witnesses* for pirate and syndicate careers; or fold into Menacing. |
| **Martial Artist** | Archives of the Empire III (Celestial Dragon Monk) | Unarmed strikes gain weapon-like damage or qualities (*unverified*). | **Add**: the natural gate talent for Echani and Teras Kasi forms: Brawling counts as an armed attack with the Fast or Impact quality. |
| **Surgical Precision** | Archives of the Empire / Up in Arms | Improves Surgery outcomes (*unverified*). | Add as a Surgery companion once medical droids and bacta are in the equipment chapter. |
| **Vice (Target)** | Archives of the Empire II | Max Willpower Bonus; Tests Willpower (Resist Target). A compulsion for a named vice. (verified from the preview) | **Add** as *Vice (Spice / Sabacc / Death sticks / Drink)*: a scoundrel's flaw-talent with teeth, and the setting's spice trade needs it. |
| **Large** | Archives of the Empire II (Ogres) | Max 1. Your Size is Large with the attached rules. (verified) | Add for Large playable species if any are allowed (Besalisk and Wookiee are Average in your species list; Hutts and Gamorreans would need it). |
| **War Wizard** | Winds of Magic | Reduces the armour penalty to channelling/casting (*unverified*). | **Add** as *Armoured Adept*: a Force user who can work in heavy armour, the Jensaarai and Mandalorian Force users. Pairs with the −1 SL per AP rule carried over from WFRP. |
| **Magical Assistant / Suffuse With (Wind)** | Winds of Magic (familiars) | Familiar talents. | Reframe as *Droid Companion*: an astromech or protocol droid that assists Tests and carries data, with ranks for better droids. Not Force-related but the mechanical hole is the same. |
| **Birth Sword** | Knights of the Grail / Archives (Bretonnian) | Inherited blade with a bonus; tied to Noble Blood. | *Heirloom Weapon*: a family lightsaber, a clan's beskar blade, with a small bonus and a story hook. Pairs with Noble Blood. |
| **Virtue of Chivalry** | Knights of the Grail (Bretonnian) | Knightly virtues with benefits and strictures. | *Code (Jedi / Mandalorian / Sith)*: a benefit while the code is kept, loss while broken. Could replace the Sin mechanic for traditions. |
| **Ward** | Tribes and Tribulations (Savage Orcs) | Protective tattoos or charms giving armour-like protection (*unverified*). | *Ritual Ward* for Dathomiri and Sith alchemists; a sorcery-chassis talent. |
| **Handmaiden of the Old Ones** | Lustria (Amazons) | Setting-specific. | Skip. |
| **Blessed by Isha / High Magic** | Lustria (Elves, Slann) | Elven high magic access. | Skip; the disciplines cover it. |
| **Uncouth Uranai** | Sea of Claws (Sea Elves) | Setting-specific social talent. | Skip. |
| **Call to Myrmidia** | Up in Arms | Myrmidian miracles access. | Skip; replaced by Tradition Adept. |

Two other things the supplements do are worth copying even though they are not talents. Up in Arms rewrites many core combat talents for the Group Advantage option; if Star Legends adopts group Advantage those rewrites are the reference. Archives of the Empire I gives the lower-class Wood Elf an unnamed talent that marks them as lower Status among their own kind; the same device would serve for clone troopers among Republic citizens or for the Hutts' slave castes.

## 3. Gaps no WFRP book fills

Reading the two lists side by side also shows where Star Wars needs talents that have no Warhammer parent at all. Suggested names and gists, to be written only once the relevant chapter exists:

Quick Draw and First Strike (above) for the duel; *Ace Pilot* (reverse a failed Pilot Test in a dogfight, the Pilot talent's combat sibling); *Evasive Manoeuvres* (use Pilot in place of Dodge for the vehicle); *Gunnery Chief* (above); *Slicer* (reverse a failed Profession (Slicer) or Codes Test, the computer equivalent of Alley Cat); *Code Breaker* (Codes bonus); *Droid Whisperer* (Creature Affinity for droids: Charm droids, read their behaviour); *Cybernetic Tolerance* (more cybernetics before penalties, the counterpart of Pure Soul for the body); *Zero-G Fighter* (ignore microgravity penalties in combat, the vacuum sibling of Enclosed Fighter); *Hyperdrive Mechanic* (Tinker for ships); *Bounty Hunter's Nose* (Track in cities and across worlds, Shadow's long-range cousin); *Force Dead* for ysalamiri-like species (a stronger Force Resistance); *Networked* for droid characters (shared awareness with other droids); *Heavy Armour Training* (reduce the Stealth and Perception penalties of trooper armour).

## Correction from the books (12 Oct, evening)

With Up in Arms, Sea of Claws and Archives of the Empire I-III on disk, the supplement talents could be checked against the text. Only these new talents exist in those five books: **Crew Commander** (Up in Arms and Sea of Claws), **Chanty** and **Master and Commander** (Sea of Claws), **Large** and **Vice (Target)** (Archives II), **Animal Assistant** (Archives III), plus Group-Advantage rewrites of Beat Blade, Distract, Drilled, Flee!, Gunner, Relentless, Reversal, Roughrider, Shieldsman and Strike to Injure (Up in Arms). The other names in the table above (Art of War, Lightning Strike, Despierta Fierro, Dog of War, Vendetta, War Dance, Warpaint, Dead Men Tell No Tales, Martial Artist, Surgical Precision, Birth Sword, Virtue of Chivalry, Ward, War Wizard, the Lustria and familiar talents) are not in these books; they belong to supplements not owned (Lustria, Winds of Magic, Tribes and Tribulations, Knights of the Grail) or to fan compilations. The SL100 talents built from them stand as our own designs. Added to chapter 07 from the books: Cadence (Chanty), Captain's Word (Master and Commander), Animal Companion (Animal Assistant); Gunnery Chief already covers Crew Commander.

## 4. Recommendation (applied 2026-10-12)

Decision: the 'add now' and 'hold' lists below are both written into the revised chapter (now 186 talents); the held six are marked *Placeholder* in their text so the missing chapters are visible. The 'skip' list stays skipped.

Add now (small, clear, fit the chapters already drafted): Strong Back, Duct Crawler (Tunnel Rat), Wild Talent (Witch!), Quick Draw (Despierta Fierro), First Strike (Lightning Strike), Tactician (Art of War), Mercenary (Dog of War), Blood Feud (Vendetta), Martial Artist, Vice (Target), Armoured Adept (War Wizard), Heirloom Weapon (Birth Sword), Droid Companion. That is thirteen talents, taking the chapter to 180.

Hold until the matching chapter exists: Gunnery Chief and Ace Pilot (ship combat), Surgical Precision (medical rules), Clan Markings and Ritual Ward (Force sorcery and tradition pages), Code (Jedi/Mandalorian/Sith) (depends on whether the Prayer chassis keeps a Sin-style track).

Skip: Fisherman, Holy Hatred, Waterman, Unshakable, Handmaiden of the Old Ones, Blessed by Isha, High Magic, Uncouth Uranai, Call to Myrmidia, Large (unless a Large species is made playable).
