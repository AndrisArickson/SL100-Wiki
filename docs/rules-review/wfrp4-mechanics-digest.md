# WFRP 4e Mechanics Digest (base for SL100 "Star Legends - Galactic Wars")

Sources: WFRP4 Rulebook (page refs = printed page numbers), fan Quick Reference v1.7 (QR), fan Player Aid (PA). The two fan sheets are condensed restatements of the rulebook; where they add house-rule options this is flagged.

## 1. Core loop

**Ten Characteristics (p.33-34):** Weapon Skill, Ballistic Skill, Strength, Toughness, Initiative, Agility, Dexterity, Intelligence, Willpower, Fellowship. Human average 30; 40+ gifted, 60+ rare. Generated as 2d10 + species modifier (Human +20 everywhere; Dwarf WS/T/Dex +30, WP +40, Ag/Fel +10; Halfling BS/Dex/WP/Fel +30, WS/S +10; Elf WS/BS/Ag/Dex/Int/WP +30, I +40). Keep rolls (+50 XP), rearrange (+25 XP), or point-buy 100 points at 4-18 each.

**Characteristic Bonus** = tens digit (S 39 → SB 3); bonuses drive Wounds, damage, encumbrance, Talent maxima, assistance limits, spell durations.

**Derived (p.33-35):** Wounds = SB + 2×TB + WPB (Halfling drops SB). Movement 4 Human, 3 Dwarf/Halfling, 5 Elf; Walk = 2×M yards, Run = 4×M. Fate/Resilience base + Extra Points to split (Human 2/1/+3; Dwarf 0/2/+2; Halfling 0/2/+3; Elf 0/0/+2). Fortune pool = Fate, Resolve pool = Resilience. Every PC picks a one-phrase **Motivation**, the refuel condition for Resolve.

**Tests (p.149-155).** d100 ≤ Skill/Characteristic (+ modifier) = success. 01-05 always succeeds, 96-00 always fails.
- *Simple Test*: yes/no. *Dramatic Test*: produces Success Levels. **SL = tens digit of (modified target) − tens digit of roll.** Auto-success ≥ +1 SL; auto-fail ≤ −1 SL. Option "Fast SL": on a pass, SL = tens die of the roll.
- *Outcomes (p.152)*: +6 Astounding Success; +4/+5 Impressive; +2/+3 Success; +0/+1 Marginal ("yes, but"); −0/−1 Marginal Failure ("no, but"); −2/−3 Failure; −4/−5 Impressive Failure; −6 Astounding Failure.
- *Difficulty (p.153)*: Very Easy +60, Easy +40, Average +20, Challenging +0, Difficult −10, Hard −20, Very Hard −30. Deliberately asymmetric; combat defaults to Challenging. Combined modifiers cap at +60/−30 (p.162).
- *Opposed (p.153)*: both roll; higher SL wins; ties to the higher tested value. Net SL = difference (combat damage). Modifiers usually hit both sides.
- *Extended (p.154)*: accumulate SL to a target; below 0 restarts at 0. Option: pass ≥ +1, fail ≤ −1.
- *Assisted (p.155)*: best roller rolls, +10 per helper; helpers need ≥1 Advance, adjacency, max = roller's Characteristic Bonus; never on resistance tests.
- *Option (p.151)*: +1 SL per full 10% a skill exceeds 100.
- Dice toolkit (p.150): **double** = both dice equal; **reverse** = swap dice (58→85); no rerolling a reroll.

**Criticals & Fumbles (p.159-160).** In combat any *successful* Melee/Ranged test on a double is a Critical → immediate Critical Wound on the target (works for the defender too in an opposed melee). Any *failed* combat test on a double is a Fumble → d100 on the Oops! table (lose 1 Wound / weapon damaged & act last / −10 next action / lose next Move / lose next Action / Torn Muscle / hit a random ally). Black-powder weapons fumbling on an even double *misfire*: weapon destroyed, full damage to primary arm. Option (p.153): extend criticals/fumbles to all tests (double = Astounding result). Impale weapons also crit on any roll ending in 0.

**Fate/Fortune, Resilience/Resolve (p.170-171).** *Fortune* (refills each session to Fate): reroll a failed test; +1 SL after rolling; pick your initiative slot. *Fate* (permanent; GM awards for extreme heroism): "Die Another Day" (out of the scene, alive) or "How Did That Miss?" (ignore the damage, fight on). *Resolve* (regained by acting on your Motivation): immunity to Psychology for a round; ignore Critical Wound modifiers for a round; remove one Condition (Prone also heals 1 Wound). *Resilience* (permanent): "I Deny You!" (refuse a mutation) or "I Will Not Fail!" (choose the dice result, auto-win an opposed test by ≥1 SL, even after failing).

**Advantage (p.164).** A momentum counter: each point = +10 to combat and Psychology tests. Gain +1 for winning any opposed test in combat, charging, attacking a Surprised/Stunned foe, assessing with a skill (Intuition, Lore, Outdoor Survival etc., capped at the relevant Bonus), wounding without an opposed test (ranged), defeating an important foe (+1/+2). Lose *all* of it on losing an opposed test, taking any Condition, or losing any Wounds; lose 1 if you gained none this round or end it outnumbered; all gone when combat ends. Spend it all to Disengage freely if you have more than your opponents (p.165). Options: cap at Initiative Bonus or a flat 2/4/10. Leadership transfers it to allies; it applies to Casting but not Channelling (p.236).

## 2. Character creation (p.24-45)

Nine steps: Species → Class & Career → Attributes → Skills & Talents → Trappings → Detail → Party → Bringing to Life → Advancement. Accepting random species/career rolls pays XP (+20 species; +50 first career roll, +25 if picked from three).

**Species (p.24-29):** Human, Dwarf, Halfling, High Elf, Wood Elf. Each gives a skill list (pick 3 at +5 Advances, 3 at +3) and Talents (some "or" choices; Humans roll 3 on the d100 Random Talent table, p.36).

**Class & Career (p.30-32, 46-52):** 8 Classes (Academics, Burghers, Courtiers, Peasants, Rangers, Riverfolk, Rogues, Warriors) × 8 Careers = 64, filtered by species. Every Career has **four levels** (Apprentice Apothecary → Apothecary → Master Apothecary → Apothecary-General), each with a Status tag, skills (8 at level 1, 4-6 added per later level), 4 Talents and Trappings. One level-1 skill is the italicised *Earning skill*.
- **Advance Scheme**: 3 Characteristics open at level 1, one more unlocked at each of levels 2, 3 and 4. You may advance skills from your current and lower levels, but only your *current* level's Talents.
- Start: 5 Characteristic Advances across the three; 40 skill Advances across the 8 career skills (max 10 each); 1 career Talent.
- **Completing a level** (p.48): Advances in *all* that level's Characteristics and in 8 of its skills: level 1 = 5, 2 = 10, 3 = 15, 4 = 20; plus ≥1 current-level Talent. Earlier Advances count.
- **Changing career (p.48-49)**: 100 XP if complete, 200 if not. Options: next (or any lower) level of the same career; level 1 of any career in your Class; same level of another Class career (GM + justification); another Class costs +100 XP. Events may jump levels. You never *have* to move.
- **Non-career advances** (p.48): double XP; non-career Talents only via Endeavours.
- **Status (p.49-52)**: Tier (Brass < Silver < Gold) + Standing (1-5), set by career level (Brass 3, Silver 1, Gold 1...). Charm +10/−10 across tiers, Intimidate +10 if higher, Gossip −10 across tiers, Leadership +10/+20 per tier above. Incognito = Brass 3. Spend about half your Standing in tier coin per day or lose 1 Standing/week (Standing 0 drops a tier). *Earning* (p.51-52): a week's work = Average (+20) Earning-skill test → Brass 2d10 pennies / Silver 1d10 shillings / Gold 1 crown *per Standing*; half on failure, nothing on −6. Starting wealth uses the same table (p.37).
- **Trappings (p.37)**: Class kit (Warriors: Clothing, Hand Weapon, Dagger, Pouch) + level-1 career Trappings + coin; also social proof of your career.

**Skills (p.117-131):** Skill = governing Characteristic + Advances. 25 *Basic* skills (Athletics, Charm, Cool, Dodge, Endurance, Intimidate, Intuition, Leadership, Melee, Perception, Stealth...) test untrained at the raw Characteristic; 20 *Advanced* skills (Channelling, Heal, Language, Lore, Pray, Ranged, Trade...) need ≥1 Advance. *Grouped* skills (Melee, Ranged, Lore, Language, Trade...) are Specialisations advanced separately; Melee (Basic) is Basic, every Ranged (x) is Advanced. Each skill entry has a "use in combat" paragraph (usually +1 Advantage, or substitute for Melee in defence).

**Talents (p.132-147):** each has *Max* (1, 2, or a Characteristic Bonus) and optionally *Tests*; a Talent tied to a Test grants +1 SL per rank on successful tests of that skill, on top of its rule. Template examples: Arcane Magic (Lore) unlocks spell learning (100 XP each for the first IntB spells, 200 for the next IntB...); Aethyric Attunement / Instinctive Diction cancel miscasts on doubles; Accurate Shot; Dual Wielder; Combat Aware; Second Sight; Bless / Invoke.

## 3. Combat (p.156-169)

**Structure:** Rounds (a few seconds) of Turns in Initiative order (highest I first; ties by Ag). Optional rolled initiative: I test for SL; d10 + I; d10 + AgB + IB. Sequence: Surprise → round start → turns → round end → repeat.

**Surprise (p.156-157):** Opposed Stealth (worst ambusher) vs Perception; losers get Surprised (no action/move, can't defend, +20 to be hit; ends at end of round or after the first attack on you). Resolve can remove it.

**Turn:** 1 Move + 1 Action in any order, plus Free Actions (anything not needing a test). Run = Action + Average Athletics for Run + SL yards. *Charge* (p.165): Move into melee, Action must be a Melee attack; +1 Advantage if the target was ≥ M yards away. *Engaged* = exchanged melee this round. *Disengage*: spend all Advantage (if you have more than your foes) and walk; else Opposed Dodge/Melee as Action; or Flee (foe gets +1 Advantage and a free attack at +20; if hit, Cool test or Broken). Option "On the Defensive": Action for +20 to one defensive skill until next turn.

**Attack sequence (p.158-159):**
1. *Roll to hit.* Melee: Opposed Melee test (defender may use Dodge or, with GM nod, Intimidate/Charm/Leadership, but only Melee can crit). Winner +1 Advantage; loser loses all. Ranged: plain Ranged test; hit = +1 Advantage; unopposable except Dodge at Point Blank or a Shield 2+. No shooting while Engaged without the Pistol quality (then the target opposes with Melee).
2. *Hit location* = reverse the to-hit roll: 01-09 Head, 10-24 Left/secondary Arm, 25-44 Right/primary Arm, 45-79 Body, 80-89 Left Leg, 90-00 Right Leg.
3. *Damage = Weapon Damage + SL* (melee SB+n; ranged fixed).
4. *Wounds lost = Damage − (TB + APs at location)*, minimum 1 (unless Undamaging). Below 0 Wounds → Critical Wound + Prone; if the overflow is less than TB, −20 on the Critical table roll.

**Combat difficulty (p.161):** +40 Point Blank, outnumber 3:1, group of 7-12; +20 Large target, Short range, group 3-6, aimed, flank/rear, outnumber 2:1, Prone target; +0 standard; −10 attacker Prone, bad footing, Long range, shoot-and-move, soft cover; −20 called shot, fog, extreme weather, off-hand, melee in darkness, medium cover; −30 arduous terrain, Extreme range, shooting in darkness, hard cover. Size (p.162): Tiny −30 to Monstrous +60. Outnumbered foes also lose 1 Advantage per round. **Range bands (p.297):** Point Blank = R/10, Short = R/2, Long = 2R, Extreme = 3R.

**Other (p.163-166):** off-hand −20 (Ambidextrous/Dual Wielder); Grapple → Entangled, then Opposed Strength for SB+SL ignoring armour; mounted +20 vs smaller targets, −20 Dodge; falling 1d10 + 3/yard, TB only; **Pursuit** = abstract Distance track, each round best pursuer SL vs worst pursued SL, 0 caught / 10+ escaped, Movement difference = bonus SL. Sudden Death option (p.173): minor foes drop at 0 Wounds.

**Wounds, Critical Wounds, Death (p.172-181).** Wounds carry no penalty; at 0 you are Prone and go Unconscious after TB rounds. Critical Wounds come from doubles or overflow damage: fresh d100 for location, then d100 on the Head/Arm/Body/Leg table (p.174-178): flat Wounds (1-5, unmodified, never chaining) plus effects — Condition stacks, Broken Bone (Minor/Major), Torn Muscle, Amputation (difficulty), Surgery-only bleeds; 00 = death. **Death**: Unconscious at 0 Wounds with more Critical Wounds than TB → die at end of round unless one is healed; helpless targets can simply be killed. Fate answers death. Options: *Pulling your blows* (no crits), *Critical Deflection* (p.299: sacrifice 1 AP to ignore the Critical). Broken bones heal in 30+1d10 days (failed Endurance = −5 permanent); torn muscles 30−TB days; amputations are permanent (hand −20 on two-handed tasks, 100 XP per −5 bought back; leg = no Dodge).

**Healing (p.181):** daily Average Endurance after sleep heals SL + TB; a rest day adds TB; Heal skill heals IntB + SL once per encounter; bandages/poultices prevent infection. "Medical Attention" and the Surgery Talent unlock marked Critical results.

**Conditions (p.167-169):** Ablaze (1d10/round), Bleeding (1 Wound/round unmodified; at 0 Wounds 10% death per stack per round), Blinded, Broken (must flee), Deafened, Entangled, Fatigued (−10 everything), Poisoned, Prone (+20 to be hit), Stunned (no Action), Surprised, Unconscious (any hit = Critical). Most carry −10 and have a removal test (Athletics, Heal, Cool, Opposed Strength, Endurance). Same conditions stack; different ones don't. Any Condition wipes Advantage; clearing a stack leaves 1 Fatigued.

**Weapon qualities (p.297-299):** Accurate (+10), Blackpowder (Cool test or Broken), Blast (n), Damaging (units die or SL), Defensive (+1 SL opposing), Distract, Entangle, Fast, Hack (damages armour), Impact (+units die), Impale (crit on 10s), Penetrating, Pistol (usable in melee), Precise (+1 SL), Pummel (Stun on head hits), Repeater (n), Shield (n), Trap Blade, Unbreakable, Wrap. **Flaws:** Dangerous (a 9 on a failed test = fumble), Imprecise (−1 SL), Reload (n) (Extended test to n SL), Slow, Tiring, Undamaging (APs doubled, no minimum Wound). Unskilled weapon group: raw WS/BS, flaws but no qualities. Damaged weapons lose 1 Damage per point. Weapon length ladder Personal→Massive (Very Long/Massive engage at 4/6 yards).

**Armour (p.299-300):** APs per location (leather 1, mail 2, plate 2; Flexible layers under rigid). Mail/plate −10 Stealth each, helms −10/−20 Perception. Qualities: Flexible, Impenetrable (odd to-hit rolls can't crit); Flaws: Partial (even rolls ignore AP), Weakpoints (Impale crits ignore AP). Option "Quick Armour": Light 1 / Medium 2 / Heavy 3 everywhere. Repairs 10% of price per AP.

## 4. Advancement, Endeavours, Corruption, Psychology, disease

**Between Adventures (p.192-201), all optional.** d100 on the **Events** table (Accused Ally, Betrayed!, tax collector takes 30%, purse cut, Suspicion of Heresy...) → spend money → up to **3 Endeavours** (1 per free week) → "Money to Burn": unbanked cash vanishes. Career levels 3-4 must take Income or drop a level.
- General Endeavours: *Banking* (d10% interest; bank fails if the withdrawal roll ≤ rate; stash = 10% loss), *Change Career* (free if complete), *Commission* (Exotic items), *Consult an Expert* (Gossip to find, pay in Favours; grants an Expert Reroll), *Crafting* (Extended Trade test; materials ¼ price; target SL Brass 5 / Silver 10 / Gold 15+), *Income* (Status earnings), *Invent!*, *Training* (non-career Characteristic/skill at career cost + tutor fee), *Unusual Learning* (non-career Talent: Hard test, +10 per prior failure).
- Class Endeavours (one step harder for outsiders): Combat Training, Foment Dissent, Latest News, Reputation (+1 Standing for one adventure), Research Lore, Study a Mark.
- **Favours** (p.198): Minor (one Endeavour), Major (2+), Significant (an adventure). Breaking one costs 1 Status.

**Corruption & Mutation (p.182-185) — the Dark Side template.** Two routes: (a) *Dark Deals*: take 1 Corruption point to reroll any test, even one already rerolled; (b) *Corrupting Influences*: Challenging Endurance (physical) or Cool (spiritual) test; Minor exposure (lesser daemon, mutant, giving in to despair/rage/excess): fail = 1; Moderate: fail 2 / marginal 1; Major (greater daemon, deal with a daemon): fail 3 / marginal 2 / success 1 / impressive 0. **Threshold**: points above WPB + TB → Challenging Endurance test; fail → lose WPB points and mutate: species table picks Body vs Mind (Humans 50/50), then d100 on the Physical table (stat tweaks: +10 Dex, Iron Skin +2 AP/−10 Ag, Inhuman Beauty +10 Fel) or Mental table (−5/−10 stats, Animosity to all, Frenzy +10 WS, Unending Malice). More mutations than TB or mental corruptions than WPB = lost to Chaos, becomes an NPC. Losing points: mutate; *Dark Whispers* (GM offers to burn a point for a treacherous act — always your choice); *Absolution* (cleanse a profane temple, pilgrimage). Resilience "I Deny You!" refuses a mutation. Deliberately slow-burn and player-driven.

**Psychology (p.190-191):** Cool test at start of round, success lasts the encounter. Animosity (−20 Fel or must attack, +1 SL), Prejudice (−10 Fel or must insult), Hatred (must destroy, +1 SL, immune to Fear from target), **Fear (n)**: Extended Cool test to n SL; until then −1 SL against the source and Cool tests to approach or avoid Broken; **Terror (n)**: one Cool test, failure = n + |SL| Broken Conditions, then Fear (n). Frenzy (WP test; immune to psychology, must charge, free melee test each round, +1 SB, Fatigued after). Custom traits (Camaraderie, Love, Phobia = Fear 1, Trauma) use ±1 SL, immunities, prescribed actions, Conditions. Advantage adds +10; Resolve buys a round of immunity.

**Disease (p.186-189):** format = Contraction (a test), Incubation, Duration, Symptoms (Blight, Fever, Flux, Gangrene, Lingering, Malaise, Nausea, Pox, Wounded — each a small Condition or stat penalty), Permanent effects. Festering Wound / Blood Rot escalate from Critical Wounds. Cures are priced with a % chance of being genuine. Exposure, thirst, starvation, suffocation (p.181) escalate from −10 stat penalties to 1d10 damage.

## 5. Magic (p.229-257) — template for a Force system

**Fiction:** the Aethyr leaks eight coloured Winds, each with a Lore (Light, Metal, Life, Heavens, Shadows, Death, Fire, Beasts); humans take one Lore; mixing winds is Dark Magic (Dhar) which corrupts; Warpstone is solidified magic. Casting uses the Language (Magick) skill and loud speech; Second Sight lets you perceive the Winds with Perception/Intuition/Track.

**Spell types:** Petty (CN 0 cantrips), Arcane (generic), Lore (need the Arcane Magic (Lore) Talent), Chaos. Format: CN, Range, Target (incl. AoE), Duration (often "WPB rounds"), effect. Lore CNs run about 4-8+. Memorising costs XP; casting from a grimoire doubles CN.

**Casting test (p.234):** Language (Magick) test; cast if SL ≥ CN; fail = nothing. **Overcasting** (p.238): every +2 SL over CN adds one more unit of Range, AoE, Duration or Targets. Critical = Minor Miscast (unless Instinctive Diction) plus a bonus: Critical Cast (adds a Critical Wound), Total Power (cast regardless of CN) or Unstoppable (undispellable). Fumble = Minor Miscast. **Magic missiles**: location = reversed casting roll; Damage = SL + WPB + spell Damage, reduced by TB and AP. Touch spells in combat need an Opposed Melee (Brawling). One instance per spell; bonuses don't stack. Advantage applies; +1 if the target was hit by the same Lore this round.

**Channelling (p.237):** Extended Channelling (WP) test to accumulate SL = CN; next round cast at CN 0 (failing that loses it all + Minor Miscast). Critical = cast next round regardless + Minor Miscast (unless Aethyric Attunement). Fumble = any double *or any roll ending in 0 above your skill* → Major Miscast. Interruption → Hard Cool test or Minor Miscast and lose the SL. −1 SL per AP on your most-armoured location and for wrong-colour garb. Ingredients (CN shillings, per spell) downgrade Major→Minor→nothing. Warpstone doubles SL but corrupts.

**Miscast tables (p.234-235):** Minor = nuisance to nasty (Deafened, glowing, WP test or +1 Corruption, 1d10 Bleeding, Prone, Fatigued, Cool or Broken, spell fires elsewhere; 96-00 escalates). Major = dangerous (area Corruption test, 1d10 unmodified Wounds, Ablaze, mute, swarm, thrown, limb frozen, 1d10 Fortune points that each cost Corruption, lose the Talent for 1d10 minutes, everyone within WPB yards takes 1d10 Wounds or your head explodes). Option: Swirling Winds, d10 for a −30 to +30 scene modifier.

**Dispelling (p.237):** Opposed Language (Magick) against an incoming spell within WP yards, once per round; persistent spells need an Extended test to the CN.

**Prayers (p.217-228)** are the simpler sibling: Bless gives six cult Blessings (+10 to a stat, small heals); Invoke gives Miracles. Challenging Pray test, no CN; +2 SL buys +6 yards / +1 target / +6 rounds (Blessings) or doubles a parameter (Miracles). Risk = **Sin points** (1-3 per broken stricture); on a fumble or when the units die ≤ Sin, roll **Wrath of the Gods** (+10 per Sin; escalating from visions through Wounds to excommunication and death-unless-Fate); each roll clears 1 Sin; Penances restore lost Talents.

## 6. Equipment & economy (p.288-309)

**Money:** 1 Gold Crown = 20 silver shillings = 240 brass pennies; written 6/8 = 6 shillings 8 pence. Each Status tier lives in one coin; cost of living ≈ half your Standing in tier coin per day. 200 coins = 1 Enc.

**Availability (p.290):** Common (always), Scarce (village 30% / town 60% / city 90%), Rare (15/30/45%), Exotic (never stocked; Commission or Craft). Reroll in a new settlement or next week in a town. Stock: village 1, town 1d10. **Haggle**: opposed; win = −10% (−20% on +6 or with Dealmaker). **Selling**: find a buyer by the same test at half list; halving your price raises buyer availability a step. **Barter** ratios 1:2:4:8 across steps. Option: let Status cover anything priced below your Standing.

**Craftsmanship (p.291-292):** each Item Quality doubles price and worsens availability one step; each Flaw halves price and improves it one step. Qualities: Durable (n), Fine, Lightweight, Practical (+1 SL on failed tests). Flaws: Ugly (−10 Fel), Shoddy (breaks on a failed double), Unreliable (−1 SL), Bulky.

**Encumbrance (p.293):** items weigh 0-3 Enc (coins 0, sword 1, backpack 2, halberd 3). Limit = SB + TB (≈6); worn items −1. Up to 2× limit: −1 M, −10 Ag, +1 travel Fatigue; up to 3×: −2 M, −20 Ag, +2; beyond: immobile. Containers have Enc and Carries values.

**Pricing model:** hand weapon 1 GC, dagger 16/–, shield 2 GC, zweihänder 10 GC, pistol 8 GC, handgun 4 GC, Hochland long rifle 100 GC (Exotic), leather jack 12/–, mail coat 3 GC, plate breastplate 10 GC, backpack 4/10. Repairs 10% of price per damage point. Tools, animals, vehicles, drugs, herbs, prosthetics and hirelings follow on p.302-309.

## 7. What a Star Wars adaptation must change

- **Winds of Magic, colour Lores, Language (Magick), grimoires, ingredients, warpstone** — setting-bound. *Reusable:* the skeleton. A WP-based Force skill test with SL ≥ a per-power "Casting Number"; overcasting (+2 SL buys range/targets/duration); Channelling as an Extended test to pre-load a big power; doubles as Critical/Fumble feeding an "overdraw" table; one instance per power; Dispel as an opposed Force test; Talent-gated learning (Arcane Magic (Lore) → Force Tradition) with per-power XP brackets on WPB; −1 SL per AP of armour; Second Sight → Force sense. Replace Lores with Force disciplines (Control/Sense/Alter or Light/Dark trees); keep "Petty" as universal CN 0 powers. Ingredients could survive as focusing crystals that downgrade a miscast; Swirling Winds → Force nexus modifiers. Miscast consequences should feed the Dark Side track.
- **Corruption & mutation** — the *mechanism* is the Dark Side: Dark Deals (a Dark Side point buys a reroll), Minor/Moderate/Major exposure tests (atrocities, powers used in anger, Sith artefacts), threshold WPB + TB, Dark Whispers, Absolution, "I Deny You!". Replace the Physical table with stigmata (Sith eyes, pallor, cybernetics); the Mental table is already Dark Side flavoured. "More mutations than TB → NPC" becomes "fallen". Drop the species Body/Mind split.
- **Bless/Invoke, Blessings/Miracles, Sin & Wrath** — no pantheon; delete. The Sin/Wrath pattern could become a Jedi/Sith Code track, but Corruption already covers it; avoid two parallel tracks.
- **Species tables** — swap in Legends species (Twi'lek, Wookiee, Rodian, Bothan, Mon Calamari, Zabrak, Trandoshan, Duros, droids) on the same 2d10 + modifier frame, Wounds formula, Fate/Resilience split, Movement, skill/Talent packs and Animosity options. Random Talent and Size tables (Hutts, rancors, AT-ATs) transfer with renames.
- **Classes & Careers, Brass/Silver/Gold** — the 8×8×4 grid, Advance Scheme, completion counts 5/10/15/20, change costs 100/200/+100, Earning skill and Trappings-as-identity are pure mechanics. Rename Classes (Academics → Scholars/Technicians, Burghers → Citizens, Courtiers → Nobles/Politicians, Peasants → Colonists, Rangers → Scouts, Riverfolk → Starship Crew, Rogues → Fringers, Warriors → Soldiers; add Force Users). Status needs non-metal names (Fringe / Citizen / Elite) but keeps Tier + Standing 1-5, the ±10 social modifiers, cost-of-living decay and the Earning table rescaled to credits.
- **Coinage** — replace with credits (peggats/wupiupi as colour). Keep Availability by settlement (outpost/colony/core world) with its percentages, Haggle −10%, selling at half, barter ratios, Quality/Flaw doubling/halving, 10%/point repairs, Commission/Crafting. Add a Restricted/Military tag.
- **Weapons & armour** — keep the damage formula, hit locations and quality/flaw vocabulary. Blasters = Blackpowder/Engineering (fixed +8/+9, Dangerous, Reload, Repeater, Pistol, Accurate; Blast for grenades); drop or restrict the "Cool test or Broken" rule; misfire → power-pack overload. Lightsabers: fixed damage, Damaging, Hack, Impale, Penetrating, Defensive, Fast, own Melee group. Stun = Undamaging + Pummel. Quick Armour bands (1/2/3) suit stormtrooper abstraction; reword Penetrating's "non-metal" as energy vs physical.
- **Horses, carts, boats, Ride/Row/Sail/Drive** — rename to Pilot (Speeder/Starfighter/Capital), Drive, Ride (Beast); mounted combat → speeder bikes; Animal Care/Training → droid maintenance. Vehicle/starship combat is a genuine gap: Pursuit (Distance track) seeds chases and dogfights, but ship Wounds/armour need a new layer.
