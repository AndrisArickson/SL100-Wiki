# Talents chapter: audit and rewrite notes (2026-10-11)

Scope: `07 New Star Wars - Talents.md` and `Talent List.md`. Output: `07 New Star Wars - Talents (revised).md` (167 talents: 148 general, 19 Force) and `Talent List (revised).md` (index generated from the revised chapter). The original files are untouched; replace them when you are happy with the revision.

## What the check found in the original

Every one of the 148 general talents was WFRP 4e text with light edits, and about forty still carried Old World residue: Nordland and Ostland (Blather), Ulric (Furious Assault), an Abbess and Greenskins (pasted into Impassioned Zeal), shillings, pennies and crowns (Dealmaker, Embezzle, Wealthy), Trade, Heal and Set Trap skill names, Melee (Fencing), Language (Magick), "non-magical Trade skills", self-flagellation (Frenzy), and twenty-one "see page N" references to WFRP pagination. Distances mixed yards and feet.

Broken entries: Briber contained Cardsharp's text; Impassioned Zeal contained Inspiring's table and example while Inspiring had no table; Criminal was cut off mid-sentence; Creature Affinity and Hyperlane Pilot each had a stray "restore a limb in 1d10 days" line pasted in; Pilot had a sentence fragment; Speedreader kept an editorial note; Jump Up switched from Agility to Athletics mid-rule; Secret Identity had "Max: Intelligence" without "Bonus"; Regeneration (Minor) was written in bestiary voice with no Max; Bookish's heading was split across two lines; Force Sensitive (Tradition) and Force Magic (Lore) were truncated; Force Ability - Sense had "Max: Intuition Bonus" (Intuition is a skill).

Talent List.md was an index of an earlier draft: 26 talents in chapter 07 were missing from it (nearly everything after Strike Mighty Blow, plus Amphibious, Eidetic Memory and Regeneration (Minor)), Combat Aware, Attractive and Lip Reading were listed twice, Force Resistance appeared under its WFRP name Magic Resistance, and twenty Force Specialty powers were listed as if they were talents.

## What the rewrite does

Every description is written fresh in Star Wars Legends terms with the mechanics preserved. Max and Tests lines are normalised to one format. Distances are in metres; money is in credits (1 shilling = 10 credits, 1 crown = 1,000 credits); page references are replaced with section pointers such as "(see Combat: Size)" that the core rules chapter should honour when it is written. Skill names follow chapter 06 (Profession, Medicine, Codes, Demolitions, Pilot (X), Creature Care, Consume Intoxicant, Melee (any) instead of Fencing).

Specific repairs: Briber restored to its own rule (bribe cost −10% per rank, minimum 10%); Inspiring and Public Speaker each carry their multiplier table; Impassioned Zeal is back to its own rule; Criminal is complete; the stray "restore a limb" lines are gone; Frenzy has no flagellation clause; Regeneration (Minor) is a Max 1 character talent; Jump Up uses Athletics throughout; Secret Identity is Max Intelligence Bonus; Concoct uses Lore (Pharmacology) and Profession (Pharmacist).

Renames: Datapad Literate and Read/Write are now **Datapad Use** (Max 1; covers reading, writing and operating datapads, terminals and the holonet; nearly universal, missing only for characters from feral, feudal or isolated origins, who cannot read or work a computer interface until they learn it). Animal Affinity is Creature Affinity everywhere including the random table. Magic Resistance is Force Resistance. Force Sensitive (Sight) is Force Sight. Instinctive Diction is Fluent Incantation; Magical Sense is Sense Sorcery; Petty Magic is Minor Sorcery; Force Magic (Lore) is Force Sorcery (Tradition); Dark Magic (Lore) is Dark Side Sorcery; Use The Force (Tradition) is Tradition Adept.

## The Force talents under the new model

The section is now split in two. Disciplines (Jedi/Sith-style) follow the decision of 11 Oct: Force Sensitive (Tradition) is the Max 1 gate that adds Use the Force to every career and unlocks the three discipline talents; Force Sense (Max Initiative Bonus), Force Control (Max Willpower Bonus) and Force Alter (Max Fellowship Bonus) are ranked, each rank giving +1 SL on successful Use the Force Tests of that discipline; the first rank of each grants its universal powers (Danger Sense, Life Detection, Sense Force; Force Speed, Reduce Injury, Concentration, Absorb Energy, Tapas; Telekinesis, Affect Mind, Kinetic Combat, Force Shield) which work like Blessings, a plain test with no Casting Number and no XP cost; Force Specialty buys named powers (the Miracle analogue) one per rank; combined powers are kept as a table. Sorcery (Sith sorcery, Nightsisters, Krath and the like) keeps the WFRP magic chassis: Force Sorcery (Tradition) with the Intelligence Bonus XP brackets, Dark Side Sorcery, Minor Sorcery, Force Attunement for channelling and Fluent Incantation for casting.

Three things in that section are my proposals, written in so the chapter reads complete, and need your yes or no:

1. Force Specialty ranks cost a flat 100 XP each (the Invoke/Miracle pattern) instead of the escalating 100/200/300 of other talents. Without this a sixth power would cost 600 XP.
2. The universal powers have light placeholder numbers (Telekinesis reach Willpower Bonus × 10 metres, Force Speed doubling Movement for SL Rounds, Force Shield as Shield (SL), Force Lightning Damage SL + Willpower Bonus). These give the Force chapter something to start from; the real numbers belong there.
3. Force Sensitive (Tradition) keeps the truncated "call on the Force" clause as: when spending a Fortune point to reroll, you may instead reduce the original roll by 1d10. The original text was cut off, so the intended size of that reduction is unknown.

Also note: Force Alter keyed to Fellowship Bonus is kept from your draft; Willpower would be the more usual choice and the three disciplines would then all key to WP or I. Pain-Sworn stays among the general talents. Force Visions, Detect Force Artefact, Force Fury, Pure Soul, Foretold and Force Resistance are unchanged in effect.

## Still open after this pass
