# EQUIPMENT

Your character starts play with equipment determined by your Origin, Faction, and Role. You can find out more about your equipment here:

# Weapons

# 

**Weapon Group**: Each Weapon is listed by its Weapon Group. If a weapon is two-handed, it is marked (2H)

**Price**: The price for an average example of the weapon.

**Enc**: The Encumbrance for the weapon.

**Availability**: The Availability of the weapon.

**Reach/Range**: The Weapon’s length, or the range of the weapon in metres.

**Damage**: The weapon’s Damage, which is added to your SL to hit.

**Qualities and Flaws**: Any Weapon Qualities or Flaws the weapon possesses

**Melee Weapon Reach**

The lengths of the weapons are progressively bigger as follows.

**Personal**: Your legs and fists, perhaps your head, and anything attached to those.

**Very Short**: Less than a foot in length.

**Short**: Up to 2 feet in length.

**Average**: Up to 3 feet long.

**Long**: Up to 6 foot long.

**Very Long**: Up to 3 metres in length; can Engage enemies up to 4 metres away, rather than just 2.

**Massive**: Anything over 10 feet long; \\

**Projectile / Energy Weapon Ranges**

S = Short

M = Medium

L = Long

E = Extreme

**Special Material note**

**Cortosis, Phrik, and Beskar** are the three main lightsaber-blocking metals — each rare, each with different ownership (Sith, Separatists, Mandalorians respectively)

**Qualities and Flaws**

**Flexible**: Flexible armour can be worn under a layer of non-Flexible armour if you wish. If you do so, you gain the benefit of both.

**Weakpoints**: The armour has small weakpoints where a weapon can penetrate if your opponent is sufficiently skilled or lucky. If your opponent has a weapon with the Impale Quality and scores a Critical, the APs of your armour are ignored.

**Cortosis Weave**: Only applied to flexible armour AP 0 - Resist 3

**Powered**: bonuses to strength, speed, agility. Replaces Medium and Heavy armour - 5,000 Cred

**Space**: Survive in space; a qualities applied to any complete set of armour - 1,000 Cred

**Active Camouflage**: a quality applied to any complete set of armour - 5,000 Cred

**Passive Camouflage**: a quality applied to any complete set of armour - 100 Cred

**Steel -&#32;**normal steel

**Durasteel** — the galaxy's workhorse alloy. An iron/carbon base mixed with heavier elements (carvanium, lommite, meleenium, neutronium, zersium), far stronger and more durable than ordinary steel yet lightweight. Withstands extreme heat, cold, and stress even in thin sheets, but can still corrode and needs maintenance. Used for everything from starship hulls and buildings to smelting pots.

**Transparisteel** — a transparent, glass-like steel with the strength of metal. Used for viewports, canopies, and windows on ships and buildings where you need to see out but keep armour-grade protection.

**Quadanium steel** — an extremely durable steel used for high-stress military surfaces. It formed the outer skin of both Death Stars and the hull/solar panels of the TIE fighter line.

**Plasteel** — a lighter, cheaper steel-plastic composite. Weaker than durasteel but easy to mould, common in consumer goods, cheaper armour, and structural components.

**Flak Vest**

- Lighter blast *vests*, by contrast, were typically simpler layered fabric/light plating rather than the crystal-gel system
- **Flak vest** — upper-torso armour focused on fragmentation protection.
- **Mk III flak vest** — a Pacnorval Defense Systems model using light durasteel plates against grenade shrapnel.
- **Battle vest** and **flexi-mesh** — other light protective vest/materials in the same family.

**Blast vests**

Light torso armour that, despite the name, gave only minimal protection against blaster bolts but was effective against shrapnel, grenade fragments, and slugthrower rounds. 

A rubbery material embedded with thousands of tiny **reflective crystals** suspended in a **semisolid gel**. Guarded against sonic, electrical, and thermal energy, as well as significant cold, on top of the blaster and impact resistance.

- **Mk 45 Protective Vest** — a popular general-issue model during the Galactic Civil War.
- **Corondexx Blast Vest** — a premium model that, on top of its physical material, generated a localized high-energy ion field to distort and disrupt incoming energy attacks (so it actually did help against blasters).
- **Heavy Kinetic Energy Vest** — a heavy blast vest worn by soldiers operating the Emperor-4 beam tube.
- **Castaan Staad armor** — a blast vest made by Castaan Armory specifically for Twi'lek warrior-caste members; closed on one side for greater flexibility at the cost of some protection.

### Cybernetics

**Iron Skin**: Metal scales or ore deposits spread across your body. You gain +2 Armour on all locations, but your Agility is permanently reduced by 10.

### Genetic Engineering:

**Lashing Tentacle**: A tentacle sprouts from your body. If it appears on a limb, it replaces it. Unarmed attacks made with the Tentacle gain the Reach and Inflict (Restrained) Traits. (Don't ask where the genetice code came from for this, you don't want to know)

**Inhuman Beauty**: Your skin becomes uncannily flawless and people cannot help but stare. You gain +10 Fellowship whenever people can see your face, and your skin never blemishes or scars.

**Horns**: Bony growths sprout from your head. Unnamed attacks made with your horns do not have the Ineffective Trait. (Many aggressive species)

**Claws**: Your fingers sprout and sharpen into talons. Your Unarmed attacks lose the Ineffective Trait and gain Rend (1). (Many aggressive species, wookiee is retractable)

**Regeneration**: You regenerate 1 Wound per hour. In addition, any lost limbs regrow after 1 d10 days. (Trandoshan)
