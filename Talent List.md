STAR WARS WFRPG - TALENT SUMMARY LIST

======================================

COMBAT TALENTS

- Accurate Shot — Deals extra ranged damage equal to Accurate Shot level.

- Battle Rage — Can end Frenzy early with a Cool Test.

- Beat Blade — Action to reduce opponent's Advantage via Melee Test.

- Berserk Charge — Gains +1 Damage per level on Charge attacks.

- Careful Strike — Can modify Hit Location result with melee or ranged.

- Combat Aware — Perception Test to ignore Surprise.

- Combat Master — Counts as extra combatants when outnumbered.

- Combat Reflexes — +10 Initiative per level for Combat Initiative.

- Dirty Fighting — +1 Damage per level with Melee (Brawling).

- Disarm — Opposed Melee Test to disarm opponent.

- Distract — Opposed Athletics/Cool Test; prevents opponent gaining Advantage.

- Drilled — Retain Advantage when an enemy causes you to lose it, if beside an ally with Drilled.

- Dual Wielder — Attack with two weapons in one Action.

- Enclosed Fighter — Ignore penalties for fighting in confined spaces; can Dodge when space is tight.

- Fast Shot — Fire ranged weapon before normal Initiative order.

- Feint — False attack; add Feint SL to a follow-up attack.

- Frightening — Gain Fear Rating 1 (+1 per extra level).

- Furious Assault — Spend Advantage or Move to make an extra attack after hitting.

- In-fighter — No penalties fighting against longer weapons; +10 to hit when in-fighting.

- Reaction Strike — Free Attack when Charged, up to level times per Round.

- Resolute — Add Resolute level to Strength Bonus when Charging.

- Reversal — Win Opposed Melee to take all opponent's Advantage instead of dealing damage.

- Riposte — Cause Damage when defending, up to Riposte level times per round (Fast weapons).

- Shieldsman — Gain Advantage equal to Shieldsman level when losing a shield defence.

- Slayer — Use opponent's Toughness Bonus as your Strength Bonus if higher; multiply melee Crit damage by size difference.

- Strike Mighty Blow — Extra melee Damage equal to talent level.

RANGED/SHOOTING TALENTS

- Deadeye Shot — Pick Hit Location for ranged attacks instead of rolling.

- Marksman — Permanent +5 to starting Ballistic Skill.

- Sharpshooter — Ignore negative modifiers for target size on Ranged Tests.

- Sniper — No penalty at Long range; half penalty at Extreme range.

STEALTH & MOVEMENT TALENTS

- Alley Cat — Reverse failed Stealth (Urban) Tests if it scores a Success.

- Catfall — Athletics Test when falling to reduce distance taken.

- Contortionist — Squeeze through gaps; benefits on Agility Tests involving body manipulation.

- Flee! — +1 Movement when Fleeing.

- Fleet Footed — +1 Movement Attribute permanently.

- Jump Up — Agility Test to stand immediately when Prone.

- Relentless — Keep Advantage when Disengaging; can Disengage even with lower Advantage.

- Rover — Bystanders get no passive Perception checks against your rural Stealth.

- Scale Sheer Surface — Climb impossible surfaces; ignore difficulty penalties.

- Shadow — Shadowing without Combined Test; use best of Perception or Stealth.

- Sprinter — +1 Movement when Running.

- Step Aside — Move 2 yards when winning a Dodge; no longer Engaged.

- Strider (Terrain) — Ignore movement penalties in specified terrain type.

SOCIAL TALENTS

- Argumentative — Use units die as SL on successful Charm (debating) Tests.

- Artistic — Natural art talent; adds Profession (Artist) to careers.

- Attractive — Use units die as SL on successful Charm (those attracted to you).

- Beneath Notice — High-Status characters ignore you; they gain no Advantage for wounding you.

- Blather — Stun target with Charm; Stunned Conditions per level of talent.

- Briber — Reduce bribe cost by 10% per level.

- Cardsharp — Use units die as SL on Gamble/Sleight of Hand at cards; draw extra cards.

- Cat-tongued — Listeners don't oppose Charm lies with Intuition.

- Commanding Presence — Lower-Status characters cannot resist your Leadership with Willpower.

- Dealmaker — Reduce/increase prices extra 10% per level when Haggling.

- Diceman — Use units die as SL on Gamble/Sleight of Hand at dice.

- Etiquette (Social Group) — Blend in socially with the chosen group.

- Gregarious — Reverse failed Gossip Tests with travellers if it scores a Success.

- Impassioned Zeal — Double Fellowship for Public Speaking about your cause.

- Inspiring — Multiply soldiers influenced by Leadership in war (see table).

- Kingpin — Ignore Status loss from the Criminal Talent.

- Linguistics — Learn any language as a Basic Skill after a month's exposure.

- Master of Disguise — Appear as someone else without a Disguise Kit.

- Master Orator — SL bonus equal to levels on Public Speaking Charm Tests.

- Menacing — SL bonus equal to levels on Intimidate Tests.

- Mimic — Replicate accents after a day's exposure.

- Noble Blood (Galactic Aristocracy) — +10% to Fellowship Tests vs lower-status; subordinates need Willpower to resist orders.

- Panhandle — Charm (Begging) Test every half hour instead of every hour.

- Public Speaker — Multiply people influenced by Charm (see table).

- Schemer — Once per session, ask GM one political question; GM answers based on secret Intelligence Test.

KNOWLEDGE & CRAFT TALENTS

- Bookish — Reverse failed Research Tests if it scores a Success.

- Carouser — Reverse failed Consume Alcohol Tests if it scores a Success.

- Concoct — Free Crafting Endeavour using Trade (Apothecary) without a Workshop.

- Craftsman (Profession) — Adds associated Profession Skill to any career entered.

- Datapad Literate — Can read and write all languages you speak.

- Field Dressing — Reverse failed Medicine Tests (Medpac/Bacta Patch) for +1 SL max.

- Field Medic — Reverse failed Profession (Medic) Tests if it scores a Success.

- Heavy Gunner — Add SL equal to level when reloading heavy weapons.

- Lip Reading — Read lips with unobstructed view via Perception Test.

- Magnum Opus — Create extraordinary unique works of art.

- Master (Profession) — Reduce required SL of Extended Profession Tests by talent level.

- Numismatics — Judge coin/credit worth without a Test; Evaluate forgeries without opposition.

- Rapid Reload — Add SL equal to level when reloading ranged weapons.

- Savant (Lore) — Auto-know pieces of information equal to level without testing.

- Savvy — Permanent +5 to starting Intelligence.

- Seasoned Traveller — Adds Lore (Local) to careers; can buy it cheaper.

- Speedreader — Reverse failed Research Tests; read pages per round equal to SL + level.

SURVIVAL & RESILIENCE TALENTS

- Coolheaded — Permanent +5 to starting Willpower.

- Hardy — Gain permanent extra Wounds equal to Toughness Bonus.

- Implacable — Ignore Wound loss from Bleeding Conditions (one per level).

- Iron Jaw — Endurance Test to immediately remove Stunned Conditions.

- Iron Will — Intimidate never causes Fear; always free to speak against intimidators.

- Lightning Reflexes — Permanent +5 to starting Agility.

- Nimble Fingered — Permanent +5 to starting Dexterity.

- Robust — Reduce all incoming Damage by +1 per level (minimum 1 Wound).

- Scavenger — Auto-forage food for self + (level) others per hour.

- Sharp — Permanent +5 to starting Initiative.

- Stone Soup — Subsist on half food; Starvation check every 3 days instead of 2.

- Stout-hearted — Extra Cool Test to remove Broken Conditions each Turn.

- Resistance (Threat) — Auto-pass first Test per session against specified threat.

- Vacuum Legs — Ignore penalties to Tests vs Space-Sickness.

PERCEPTION & AWARENESS TALENTS

-------------------------------

- Acute Sense (Sense) — Detect normally imperceptible details with a chosen sense.

- Combat Aware — Perception Test to ignore Surprise in melee.

- Detect Force Artefact — Intuition Test to detect Force-attuned artefacts; extra SL reveals properties.

- Hunter's Eye — Auto-hunt food for self + (level) others with time and equipment.

- Magical Sense — Intuition Test to detect spellcasters nearby.

- Night Vision — See clearly 20 yards per level in natural darkness; extend light radius.

- Nose for Trouble — Intuition Test to spot troublemakers even when normally blocked; ignore Surprise from them.

- Orientation — Automatically know direction from environmental cues.

- Perfect Pitch — Perfect note replication; adds Entertain (Sing) to careers.

- Sixth Sense — GM warns of danger via secret Intuition Test; may ignore Surprise on Intuition Test.

CRIMINAL & UNDERWORLD TALENTS

- Break and Enter — +1 Damage per level against inanimate objects when forcing entry.

- Criminal — Active criminal; earns more but lowers perceived Status.

- Embezzle — Opposed Intelligence Test to skim money from employer undetected.

- Fast Hands — No passive Perception vs Sleight of Hand; +10×level to touch Melee (Brawling).

RIDING & PILOTING TALENTS

--------------------------

- Crack the Whip — Controlled mount/vehicle gains +1 Movement when Fleeing/Running with a goad.

- Hyperlane Pilot — Auto-pass easy hyperlane navigation Tests; never test with appropriate Lore Skill.

- Pilot — Reverse failed dangerous-space Pilot Tests for +1 SL max.

- Roughrider — Direct mount or vehicle to take an Action without a Test.

- Spacer — Ignore space-travel penalties; count as two crew for minimum piloting needs.

LUCK & FATE TALENTS

- Foretold — A Force seer foretold your death; next character gets half XP bonus if death matches.

- Luck — Maximum Fortune Points = Fate points + Luck levels.

FORCE TALENTS

Force Attunement — No Miscast on doubles with successful Channel Tests.

- Force Fury — +1 Damage with Force powers per level; risks Dark Side Influence.

- Force Magic (Lore) — Learn spells from a chosen Lore; cost scales with Intelligence Bonus.

- Force Sensitive (Sight) — Can perceive the currents of the Force; gain the Sight.

- Force Sensitive (Tradition) — Attuned to one Force tradition; unlocks Force Ability Talents.

- Force Visions — Auto-detect Force nexus; Intuition Test for visions of events there.

- Instinctive Diction — No Miscast on doubles with successful Language (Magick) Tests.

- Magic Resistance — Oppose all incoming Force spells with Willpower; cannot learn magic Talents.

- Petty Magic — Manifest and memorise Petty spells equal to Willpower Bonus.

- Pure Soul — Gain extra Corruption points per level before testing for Dark Side fall.

- Use The Force (Tradition) — Deeply attuned to tradition; buy extra Tradition powers for 100 XP each.

- Dark Magic (Lore) — Access Dark Side magic; gain Corruption per spell learned.

FORCE ABILITIES (DISCIPLINES)

- Force Ability - Sense (max: Initiative Bonus) — Perceiving the Force: Danger Sense, Life Detection, Sense Force.

- Force Ability - Control (max: Willpower Bonus) — Mastery of self: Force Speed, Reduce Injury, Concentration, Absorb Energy, Protection from Cold.

- Force Ability - Alter (max: Fellowship Bonus) — Shaping the world: Telekinesis, Affect Mind, Kinetic Combat, Force Shield.

FORCE SPECIALTIES

Require Force Ability - Alter:

- Projective Telepathy — Send thoughts/images into another's mind at any distance.

- Force Dominate — Plant hard-to-resist commands in individuals.

- Force Lightning — Generate destructive electrical arcs (Dark Side).

- Force Illusion — Create convincing sensory hallucinations.

Require Force Ability - Control:

- Accelerate Healing — Speed recovery from wounds in yourself.

- Force Hibernation — Slow metabolism to survive extreme conditions.

- Detoxify Poison — Purge venom through cellular-level Force focus.

- Force of Will — Reinforce mind against domination and Sith intrusion.

Require Force Ability - Sense:

- Farsight — See distant places or possible futures.

- Postcognition / Psychometry — Read Force imprints on objects or places.

- Sense Personal Force — Detect Force strength and alignment in another being.

- Receptive Telepathy — Passively read surface thoughts or emotions.

- Beast Languages — Understand animals' emotional intent through Force empathy.

COMBINED DISCIPLINE FORCE POWERS (require multiple abilities)

- Battle Meditation (C+S+A) — Bolster allies' coordination; sap enemy will.

- Force Meld (C+S+A) — Link multiple Force users into shared battle-awareness.

- Tutaminis (C+A) — Absorb raw energy (including Force lightning) harmlessly.

- Force Cloak (C+A) — Bend light around oneself to become invisible.

- Force Healing Others (C+S+A) — Extend healing energy to repair another's body.

- Plant Surge (S+A) — Explosively accelerate plant growth.

- Cleanse Soul (C+A) — Use life essence and purity to reduce another's corruption.

SOCIAL STANDING TALENTS

- Ambidextrous — Off-hand penalty reduced to –10 (or 0 if taken twice).

- Attractive — Units die as SL on Charm vs those attracted to you.

- Creature Affinity — Non-aggressive Bestial creatures are automatically calm around you.

- Hatred (group) — Consumed with hatred for a chosen group (game effects per page 190).

- Fearless (Enemy) — Average Cool Test to ignore Intimidate/Fear/Terror from specified enemy.

- Secret Identity — Maintain a false Career/Status identity per level.

MISCELLANEOUS

- Pain-Sworn — Maintain Wounds per level via daily meditation; enter Frenzy immediately if Frenzy Talent held.

- Frenzy — Can enter Frenzy; must self-flagellate daily.

- Small — Shorter than most; see Size rules in Chapter 12.

- Lip Reading — Read lips via Perception Test with clear view of speaker's face.
