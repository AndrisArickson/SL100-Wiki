Force Influence

Force Influence impacts your ability to use the force. Having a strong light side, or strong dark side influence changes how powerful or insightful your connection to The Force is, and how it impacts you personally and at extreme levels, it can impact you physically.

**Dark Side Influence**

Examples of 1 point of dark side influence

- Accidentally killing a sentient being or complex life form in strong emotion - anger, fear, aggression
- Per month; Your dark side apprentice still follows you and wants to kill you. They havent succeeded yet!

**Examples of 10 points**

- Personally killing a sentient being or complex life form in strong emotion - anger, fear, aggression
- Causing (by indirect decision or direct action) torture or suffering to a sentient being, ie committing someone to slavery
- Damning the future of a group of people; to despair and hopelessness
- Taking on an apprentice to the Dark Side of the Force
- Trading in drugs which are known to cause bad affects (glitterstim)

**Light Side Influence**

Examples of 1 point of light side influence

- Saving a life of a sentient being or complex life form. Where there is life, there is hope.
- Healing someone from a serious and / or long term illness - hope when before, there was none. Only darkness.
- Per month; In good standing with your apprentice to the Light Side of the Force

Examples of 10 points

- Saving the future of a group of people; providing hope
- Freeing a sentient being from torture or suffering, ie freeing someone from slavery
- Taking on an apprentice to the Light Side of the Force
