## CREATING A CHARACTER

A *Star Legends – Galactic Wars* character is built in layers. Your Characteristics are the raw material; your **species** gives you a body and a heritage; your **Origin** is the kind of world that raised you; your **career** is what you have done with your life so far; and your **Faction** is who you answered to before your Patron found you. Work through the steps in order:

| Step | What you do | Wiki page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Generate your ten Characteristics (roll or point-buy). | [[SL100:Characteristics|Characteristics]] |
| 2 | Choose or roll your **species**: Characteristic modifiers, species talents, Movement, Destiny, Resilience and Extra Points. | [[SL100:Species|Species]] |
| 3 | Choose or roll your **Origin** world: +5 to one Characteristic, +5 to one of three others, and skill advances from its list of twelve. | [[SL100:Origin|Origin]] |
| 4 | Choose your **class and career**, and start at its first level: skills, talents, trappings and Status. | [[SL100:Careers|Careers]] |
| 5 | Choose your **Faction**: the organisation or tradition you came from, and your Patron. | [[SL100:Faction|Faction]] |
| 6 | Work out your **Wounds**, **Movement**, **Destiny and Fortune**, **Resilience and Resolve**, and pick a **Motivation**. | [[SL100:Characteristics|Characteristics]] |
| 7 | Spend any bonus XP from step 1 on Advances in your career. | [[SL100:Careers|Careers]] |
| 8 | Add the details: name, age, appearance, and your character's first goals. | — |

## CHARACTERISTICS

Characteristics represent a character’s raw potential and natural physical and mental attributes, such as strength, agility, intelligence, and combat abilities. Characters in *Star Legends – Galactic Wars* have ten Characteristics, which are used along with Skills when making Tests. Characteristics range in value from 1 to 100, with the Human average being between 30 and 40. Well practiced or augmented individuals exceed this average, with the towering Wookiees having average Strength scores in the 60s and beyond, and the mental giants of the Arkanians having an Intelligence of 60 and greater. Since Characteristics set the base target number for all Tests, high Characteristics are always more favourable than low characteristics.

### CHARACTERISTIC BONUS

The first or ‘tens place’ digit of each Characteristic is its Characteristic Bonus. Characteristic Bonuses are used in a variety of ways, such as determining weapon Damage, calculating Wounds, and as Requirements and maximums for Talents.

**Example**: Strength (S) 39 equates to a Strength Bonus (SB) of 3. Willpower (WP) 51 equates to a Willpower Bonus (WPB) of 5.

### DETERMINE CHARACTERISTICS

To determine your Starting Characteristics:

- Step 1: Roll 2d10 + 20 for each of the ten Characteristics in order and make a note of the results. If you feel your character would be improved if you swapped some of the results between Characteristics, go to Step 2 below. If you are happy to keep your random results, note them on your character sheet, gain 50 XP, and move on to 2) Species.
- Step 2: Rearrange the ten numbers rolled in Step 1, assigning each to a different Characteristic. If you’re happy with the new values, record them on your character sheet, gain 25 XP, and move on to 2) Species. If not, go to Step 3 below.
- Step 3: If you’re still not happy with your results, roll again and swap the Characteristics around until you get something you like, but don't gain any bonus XP.
- Alternatively, instead of rolling, you may choose to assign points. Each Characteristic begins at 20 and you have 110 points to allocate across the ten Characteristics as you prefer, with a minimum of 4 and a maximum of 18 allocated to any single Characteristic. You do not gain bonus XP with this option.

Your **species** and your **Origin** world then modify these numbers. These modifiers are applied to your Starting Characteristics and do not count as Advances. At the end of character creation, you can spend XP to purchase Advances to further increase a Characteristic.

## DERIVED ATTRIBUTES

### WOUNDS

Wounds measure how much punishment you can take before you are seriously hurt. Calculate them once your species and Origin modifiers are applied:

**Wounds = Strength Bonus + (2 × Toughness Bonus) + Willpower Bonus**

Talents such as Hardy add to this total. Whenever one of these Bonuses changes, recalculate your Wounds.

**Example:** A Trandoshan with S 41, T 45 and WP 33 has 4 + (2 × 4) + 3 = **15 Wounds**.

### MOVEMENT

Your species gives you a Movement (M) value; most humanoids have Movement 4. Movement sets how far you can travel in a single round. All distances in *Star Legends – Galactic Wars* are in **metres**.

| Movement | Walk (metres per round) | Run (metres per round) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 6 | 12 |
| 4 | 8 | 16 |
| 5 | 10 | 20 |
| 6 | 12 | 24 |
| 7 | 14 | 28 |

Walk is 2 × Movement and Run is 4 × Movement.

### DESTINY AND FORTUNE

Some beings are touched by destiny. **Destiny** is a permanent measure of how far the galaxy bends around you; **Fortune** is the luck you can spend from session to session. At the start of each session your Fortune points are refilled to equal your Destiny.

Spend a **Fortune** point to:

- reroll a failed Test (you can only reroll a given Test once);
- add +1 SL to a Test after you have rolled it;
- choose where you act in the Initiative order for one round.

Spend a point of **Destiny** – permanently reducing both Destiny and your Fortune limit – when death or disaster is certain:

- **Not Today:** you survive whatever would have killed you, but you are out of the action for the rest of the scene; the GM decides how you escaped.
- **That Was Close:** ignore all damage and effects from one attack or hazard and carry on.

The GM may award new Destiny for great sacrifice or truly heroic deeds.

### RESILIENCE AND RESOLVE

**Resilience** is your inner strength and refusal to be broken; **Resolve** is how much of it you can draw on right now. Your Resolve is refilled to equal your Resilience whenever you act in pursuit of your **Motivation**, at the GM's discretion.

Spend a **Resolve** point to:

- ignore all Psychology (fear, terror and the like) for one round;
- ignore the modifiers from your Critical Wounds for one round;
- remove one Condition (removing Prone also restores 1 Wound).

Spend a point of **Resilience** – permanently reducing both Resilience and your Resolve limit – to:

- **I Will Not Fail:** choose the result of your dice for one Test, rather than rolling; for an Opposed Test you win by at least +1 SL.
- **Not My Path:** refuse a permanent, unwanted change to your mind or body, such as a dark side corruption effect.

### MOTIVATION

Every character has a **Motivation**: a single word or short phrase describing what drives them, such as *Loyalty*, *Freedom*, *Credits*, *Redemption* or *The Order*. Acting in line with your Motivation in a meaningful way lets you refill Resolve. Agree your Motivation with your GM; you can change it when your character's outlook genuinely changes.

### SPECIES VALUES AND EXTRA POINTS

Each species lists starting values for **Destiny**, **Resilience** and **Extra Points**. Divide the Extra Points between Destiny and Resilience as you choose. Your starting Fortune equals your Destiny, and your starting Resolve equals your Resilience.

**Example:** A Human has Destiny 2, Resilience 1 and 3 Extra Points. Choosing to put 2 into Resilience and 1 into Destiny gives Destiny 3 (Fortune 3) and Resilience 3 (Resolve 3).
