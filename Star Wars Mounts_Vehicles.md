**Star Wars Mounts & Vehicles**

Living Mounts (Air, Land, Sea) use the same creature stat line as the Star Wars Bestiary - Movement, Weapon Skill, Ballistic Skill, Strength, Toughness, Initiative, Agility, Dexterity, Intelligence, Willpower, Fellowship, Wounds - plus Traits and Optional add-ons. Each mount also lists Cost to buy, Carries (the Encumbrance points of rider, tack, and cargo it can bear - 1 Enc = 10kg, matching the Starship Compendium's conversion), and Availability (Common, Scarce, Rare, Exotic, or Military/Restricted).

*Tanks and Walkers are vehicles, not creatures, so they use the Starship Compendium's table format instead: Era/Faction, Size, Crew, Cargo capacity in Encumbrance points, Cost, Speed (in km/h rather than MGLT, since these are ground vehicles with no hyperdrive or sublight space engine), Weapons, and Availability.*

**Incom T-16 Skyhopper**

- **Type**: high-performance airspeeder made by Incom Corporation
- **Cost**: 14,500 credits (new), 6,500 credits (used)
- **Length**: 5.2 meters; height 10.4 meters
- **Maximum speed**: 1,200 km/h
- **Maximum altitude**: 300 km; its ion drive and fully pressurized cockpit allowed it to operate at trans-orbital altitudes
- **Engines**: E-16/x ion engine and a DCJ-45 repulsorlift generator
- **Crew**: 1 pilot, 1 passenger
- **Armament**: civilian versions were armed with pneumatic cannons and fitted with targeting lasers. Many law-enforcement agencies and planetary militaries used modified T-16s because they performed well
- **Military variant**: the MT-16 was a T-16 modified by the New Republic for combat, armed with a single blaster cannon, with a flight ceiling of two hundred kilometers
- **Famous owner**: Luke Skywalker, who raced it through Beggar's Canyon with Biggs Darklighter and shot womp rats from it
