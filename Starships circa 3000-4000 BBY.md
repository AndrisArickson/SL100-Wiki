**Ancient Starships**

- **Xim-era ships (25,100 BBY)** predate the modern hyperdrive entirely. The Tionese used fixed-position hyperspace beacon networks — think lighthouses rather than an onboard engine — so they have no standard Class rating and couldn't venture far outside their beacon grid. A note on the Xim era (\~25,100 BBY): starfighter-scale craft from that period are not individually named or statted in any Legends source. The Tionese used small "battlebird" and "cutters" in mixed squadrons, but no single-seat fighter specs survive.
- **The very ancient ships** (Kumauri, Cal-class) have almost no surviving Legends stat data. Their entries are grounded in what Legends sources say about their role and era.
- The **Inexpugnable-class** is the oddest ship in the galaxy at that period — a 3,100-metre disc that functioned more like a space station than a warship, with only 6 ever built.
- The **Harrower-class** technically falls right at the 3,000 BBY boundary (first production \~3,712 BBY) so I've included it as it comfortably predates your cutoff.

**Star Saber XC-01**

- **First service:** \~4,000 BBY (Great Sith War)
- **Class:** Starfighter
- **Crew:** 1 (pilot only)
- **Passengers:** None
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Notes:** Republic Fleet Systems; 13m long; fast but dangerously unresponsive — its low maneuverability-to-speed ratio and slow control stick reaction gave it a reputation as dangerous and unwieldy, so it was largely flown by Jedi who could compensate with the Force. Retired when the Aurek entered service.

***

**Aurek-class Tactical Strikefighter**

- **First service:** \~3,976 BBY (Mandalorian Wars), designed during the post-Great Sith War restoration
- **Class:** Strikefighter
- **Crew:** 1 (pilot only)
- **Passengers:** None
- **Hyperdrive:** Class 2
- **Notes:** Republic Fleet Systems; 9.2m long; delta-wing with variable-geometry wings. Armed with two heavy laser cannons and two proton torpedo launchers capable of holding six torpedoes; easily refuelable and rearmable, driving down costs. Served as the Republic's primary "hyperspace snub" for nearly 4,000 years through constant upgrades. The direct spiritual predecessor of the Rebel Alliance A-wing. [Fandom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurek-class_tactical_strikefighter)

**S-250 Chela-class Starfighter**

- **First service:** \~3,956 BBY (Jedi Civil War)
- **Class:** Starfighter
- **Crew:** 1 (pilot only)
- **Passengers:** None
- **Hyperdrive:** Yes (class unspecified in sources)
- **Notes:** A loser in the "Generation Tomorrow Starfighter" competition against the Aurek, it was still purchased in small numbers by the Republic. Armed with twin laser cannons and missile launchers; often flown by higher-ranking pilots and squad leaders. Carried aboard Inexpugnable-class command ships (24 per ship). [Fandom](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-250_Chela-class_starfighter)
