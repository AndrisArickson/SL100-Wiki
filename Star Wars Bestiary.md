**Star Wars Bestiary**

Each creature uses the standard WFRP 4e characteristic line — Movement, Weapon Skill, Ballistic Skill, Strength, Toughness, Initiative, Agility, Dexterity, Intelligence, Willpower, Fellowship, and Wounds — plus a Traits list (always active) and an Optional list (situational add-ons a GM may bolt on for a tougher or reflavoured specimen). Traits without a note are used exactly as their WFRP equivalents. New, Star Wars-specific traits are marked with an asterisk (\*) and defined in the glossary at the end of this document.

*Armour values represent physical Armour Points as normal; where a creature's hide or plating specifically shrugs off lightsabers or blaster fire, that's called out in its Traits.*

## New Traits Glossary

*Traits marked with \* above are new for this bestiary. Existing WFRP traits (Bestial, Armour, Fear, Terror, Undead, Ethereal, Construct, Regeneration, Corruption, etc.) function exactly as written in the Core Rulebook.*

**Tunneller:&#32;**Can move through loose sand or soft rock at full Movement, and may attempt to ambush from below (Surprise) any target standing on suitable ground.

**Vacuum-Adapted:&#32;**Immune to the effects of vacuum, extreme cold, and suffocation; unaffected by zero-atmosphere environments.

**Power Drain:&#32;**In place of a Bite or Weapon attack, may drain a ship system, droid, or powered device instead of a living target, disabling it until repaired.

**Force Sense:&#32;**Automatically detects Force-sensitive creatures within Initiative Bonus x 10 yards, and gains +10 to WS and Initiative Tests when attacking a Force-sensitive target.

**Pack Tactics:&#32;**Gains +10 to WS Tests when attacking the same target as two or more other creatures with this trait.

**Fast:&#32;**May re-roll failed Agility Tests made to determine turn order or to avoid being surprised.

**Multiple Limbs:&#32;**Makes two additional attacks per round at no penalty, representing extra limbs or claws.

**Pounce:&#32;**May combine a Charge action with a Bite or Weapon attack for +1 SL if the target is unaware.

**Grapple:&#32;**Gains +20 to Athletics Tests made to grapple or restrain a target.

**Untraceable:&#32;**Force-based precognition, danger sense, or divination abilities suffer a –20 penalty when used to predict this creature's actions.

**Sonic Roar:&#32;**A Breath weapon that deals no physical damage but forces all creatures within range to make a Cool Test or become Broken; can also shatter unshielded viewports and comparably fragile materials.

**Swallow:&#32;**On a Critical Hit with its Bite, may instead swallow a Size (Average) or smaller target whole; the target takes ongoing damage each round until cut or blasted free.

**Infected (Rakghoul Plague):&#32;**A creature reduced to 0 Wounds by this bite must pass a Toughness Test or begin transforming into a rakghoul over the following days.

**Lightsaber-Resistant Hide:&#32;**Lightsabers treat this creature's Armour as though it were 2 points higher than listed.

**Corruption:&#32;**This creature radiates a powerful dark-side presence; Force-sensitive creatures within earshot must make a Cool Test or suffer Dark Side Influence.

**Immunity (Force Powers):&#32;**Cannot be targeted by Force powers that require a Willpower-vs-Willpower contest; other Force powers affect it at –20.

**Force-Bound:&#32;**Can only be harmed by lightsabers, the Force, or other creatures with the Ethereal or Force-Bound trait.

**Possession:&#32;**May attempt to possess an unconscious or willing creature instead of attacking, taking control of their body (GM's discretion on contest mechanics).

**Deflector Shield:&#32;**Ignores the first 4 points of damage from ranged attacks each round; the shield can be overloaded (GM's discretion) by sustained fire or an Ion-trait weapon, disabling it for the rest of the encounter.

**Networked:&#32;**Gains +10 to WS or BS when at least one other creature with this trait is within Initiative Bonus x 10 yards and can see the same target.

**Sonic Screech:&#32;**A short-range shriek or cry; all creatures within range must pass a Cool Test or become Broken for one round. Deals no physical damage.

**Cold-Adapted:&#32;**Suffers no penalties from extreme cold and cannot be tracked by scent across snow or ice.

**Blaster-Resistant Hide:&#32;**Ranged blaster and other energy-weapon attacks treat this creature's Armour as though it were 3 points higher than listed; melee and lightsaber attacks are unaffected.

**Flight (Orbital Drop):&#32;**May enter combat directly from high altitude or orbit, ignoring fall damage and gaining a Surprise round against targets who haven't detected the approach.

**Scavenger:&#32;**Can consume inorganic material (wiring, plating, machine parts) as though it were food, without ill effect.

**Impale:&#32;**On a Critical Hit with this attack, the target also begins Bleeding (or the GM's equivalent ongoing-damage condition) until treated.

**Reflective Hide:&#32;**On a failed or fumbled ranged blaster attack against this creature, the GM may rule the bolt deflects back at the attacker or another nearby target.

**Shadow-Cloak:&#32;**While stationary or moving slowly, can only be detected with a Perception Test at –20; Force-based detection (danger sense, Force sight, and the like) suffers the same penalty unless the observer is a trained Jedi or Sith.

**Cold Vulnerability:&#32;**Takes 1 Wound of damage for every hour spent in freezing conditions without shelter or an external heat source.

**Force Neutral Zone:&#32;**Projects a stationary Force-null bubble (radius Toughness Bonus x 10 metres) in which Force powers cannot be used or targeted; multiple sources in proximity extend the radius further.

**Rapid Breeder:&#32;**Left unchecked, a population of this creature can double within days; for any encounter not resolved quickly, the GM may add further specimens to represent unchecked breeding.

**Force Conduit:&#32;**Reserved for rare, named specimens. Can speak, open an instantaneous path through the Force between two points ('a hyperspace tunnel'), and use Telekinesis-tier Force powers at the GM's discretion.

**Climber:&#32;**Gains +20 to Athletics Tests made to climb, and may climb at full Movement without a Test.

**Aquatic:&#32;**Moves and fights at full Movement while submerged, and suffers no penalty for fighting underwater.

**Fire-Adapted:&#32;**Immune to fire, heat, and exposure to lava.

**Ambush:&#32;**Gains a bonus (+20 WS, GM's discretion) on the first attack made from concealment or after remaining motionless before striking.

**Dark Side Venom:&#32;**A creature bitten by this attack that fails a Toughness Test suffers Dark Side Influence in addition to any other injury.

**Force Disruption:&#32;**Any Force-sensitive creature within Initiative Bonus x 10 yards attempting to use a Force power without embracing the dark side suffers a –20 penalty.

**Pheromones:&#32;**As a Free Action once per round, may attempt a Charm-like Test to influence the mood of one nearby creature; the target may resist with a Willpower Test.

**Sarlacc Digestion:&#32;**A creature swallowed by this attack is not slain outright — it is digested alive over years rather than minutes, leaving a window in which rescue remains possible.

**Hyperspace Jump:&#32;**Can travel through hyperspace unaided, over distances and durations the GM determines.

**Hive Mind:&#32;**Every creature with this trait sharing the same hive is aware of what any other member currently senses (within a range the GM sets); none can be caught by Surprise so long as any single member of the hive isn't.

**Corrosive Touch:&#32;**A successful unarmed or bite attack permanently degrades a target's Armour by 1 point, or damages exposed equipment at the GM's discretion.

**Blind Senses:&#32;**Has no functional eyesight, but automatically detects motion, heat, or scent within its territory and suffers no penalty operating in darkness.

**Curl-and-Strike:&#32;**May curl into a defensive ball as a half action (treat as Armour +2 while curled), then unfurl and make an immediate attack at +10 to hit against a target that hasn't detected the approach.

**Sonic Vulnerability:&#32;**Sonic or ultrasonic weapons and effects gain +10 to hit this creature and deal +1 SL of damage.

**Hive-Bound:&#32;**Suffers a –10 penalty to WP Tests made to act directly against the will of its hive's ruling queen or caste.

**Sticky Fingers:&#32;**May attempt to steal a small unattended or held item with an opposed Agility Test, usable once per round as a free action.

**Camouflage:&#32;**Gains +10 to Stealth-type Tests while in its natural habitat.

**Filter Feeder:&#32;**Feeds on gas, plankton, or similarly passive matter rather than living prey; has no natural Bite or Weapon attack and will not initiate hostility unless threatened.

**Sessile:&#32;**Cannot move from its position under any circumstance; Movement is always 0.ay climb at full Movement without a Test.
